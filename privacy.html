Privacy Policy

Updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Britland Academy app and website (britlandacademy.co.uk), run by Cruz e Trento Cursos de Idiomas, use and why. It applies to both the English app and the Spanish app, which share the same account and the same database. The app lets students study, follow their progress, and see and reschedule their lessons.

What data we use

  • Registration: name, e-mail, date of birth (for the birthday greeting) and a profile picture, if you upload one.
  • Study: exercise answers, scores, points, day streak and lesson progress.
  • Lessons: dates, times and titles of your lessons, and the requests for a new lesson or a reschedule that you make in the app.
  • Payments: the amount and status of the monthly fee and receipts you send. We do not store card details.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, your device code, used to send lesson and study reminders.

Use of Google Calendar

The teacher connects a calendar that belongs to the school ("Aulas Britland") to the app through Google sign-in. The app reads the events in that calendar to show each student only their own lessons, and creates, changes or cancels events in that calendar when the teacher approves a lesson or reschedule request.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Calendar data is used only to display and manage lessons. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties.

The app does not access students' personal Google Calendar. Students do not need to sign in with a Google account to use the app.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. It is stored with our database provider (Supabase) and is seen only by you and the teacher. We may use e-mail and notification services only to deliver app messages.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are a student. When you leave the school, you can ask for your data to be deleted.

Your rights

Under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD), you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw permissions such as notifications. To do so, write to the contact below.

Security

Connections use HTTPS and access to data requires a login. Each student sees only their own information.
