# Simulado A · Stage 4 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

A motorbike is quicker than a bicycle. The opposite of “ill” is “well”. Cars start moving when traffic lights change from red to green. The future of “I go” is “I will go”. Goodbye! I’m going now. I’ll see you in two weeks’ time. We call a bad dream a nightmare. We call the arms of a tree branches. Americans call the sport that British people call football “soccer”. A crime is an action which is against the law. We use red for danger. We must look both ways before we walk across the road. The three forms of “give” are “give, gave, given”. My dad never loses his way when he drives in London.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What can we say instead of “a lot of”?  a) a little  b) lots of  c) a few → **b** (L45)
2. What does a hairdresser use to cut hair?  a) scissors  b) a brush  c) soap → **a** (L47)
3. What colour’s blood?  a) blue  b) green  c) red → **c** (L47)
4. What’s the opposite of “life”?  a) death  b) heaven  c) hell → **a** (L48)
5. What’s the plural of “wife”?  a) wifes  b) wives  c) wive → **b** (L53)
6. What’s the opposite of “guilty”?  a) clever  b) dead  c) innocent → **c** (L56)
7. What do we call somebody who paints paintings?  a) a pilot  b) a painter  c) a driver → **b** (L57)
8. What happens when somebody drinks too much alcohol?  a) they get drunk  b) they get married  c) they get lost → **a** (L59)
9. What’s an informal word for “man”?  a) a bike  b) guy  c) a wife → **b** (L63)
10. What’s another word for “intelligent”?  a) clever  b) quick  c) guilty → **a** (L56)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. I’m not Mr Jackson. My ____ is Davis, and my first name is Jackson. → **surname** (L45)
2. Harry, stop the car! The ____ light’s red! → **traffic** (L46)
3. OK, class. Please ____ over the page in your books and do Exercise 2. → **turn** (L48)
4. Teacher: “Please ____ the noun ‘practice’, Amy.” Amy: “P – R – A – C – T – I – C – E” → **spell** (L50)
5. Some students in my class failed the exam, but I ____ it easily! → **passed** (L51)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. Which radio station are you listening ____ ?  a) at  b) –  c) to → **c** (L45)
2. How much physical exercise ____ each week?  a) can you do  b) have you got  c) do you make → **a** (L46)
3. George hates speaking ____ public.  a) before  b) in  c) to → **b** (L47)
4. If you’re feeling tired, I suggest you ____ some coffee.  a) drinking  b) drink  c) to drink → **b** (L48)
5. Why did you decide ____ chocolates for me? It’s not my birthday.  a) buying  b) you bought  c) to buy → **c** (L49)
6. These chocolates are ____ sweet for me.  a) too  b) too many  c) too much → **a** (L53)
7. Did Alicia enjoy ____ in the river last weekend?  a) swimming  b) to swim  c) that she swam → **a** (L56)
8. We walked ____ the river for about an hour before stopping to have lunch.  a) through  b) along  c) across → **b** (L61)
9. Why are your clothes on the floor? Please ____ them up.  a) throw  b) drop  c) pick → **c** (L62)
10. I don’t like monkeys. I’m afraid ____ them.  a) for  b) from  c) of → **c** (L63)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. How many mistakes did you do in the last dictation? → **How many mistakes did you make in the last dictation?** (L45)
2. You can give James some medicine, but he wont take it. He doesn’t think he’s ill. → **You can give James some medicine, but he won’t take it. He doesn’t think he’s ill.** (L47)
3. The weather’s warm now, but tomorrow will be colder, and it will be no sun until midday. → **The weather’s warm now, but tomorrow will be colder, and there will be no sun until midday.** (L48)
4. We mustn’t forget to get more eggs when we make the shopping tomorrow. → **We mustn’t forget to get more eggs when we do the shopping tomorrow.** (L52)
5. Goodbye! I’m going now. I’ll see you in two week’s time. → **Goodbye! I’m going now. I’ll see you in two weeks’ time.** (L46, adaptado)
6. I’m hungry enough to have lunch now. Therefore, I want to look inside this clothes shop first. → **I’m hungry enough to have lunch now. However, I want to look inside this clothes shop first.** (L50, adaptado)
7. I’ll be in the office tomorrow. I hope you to be there too. → **I’ll be in the office tomorrow. I hope you will be there too.** (L51, adaptado)
8. Dinesh never says hello to us. I make him very impolite. → **Dinesh never says hello to us. I find him very impolite.** (L53, adaptado)
9. If you keep up trying, you will succeed. → **If you keep on trying, you will succeed.** (L54, adaptado)
10. Not everyone can work like a teacher. Teaching teenagers is hard! → **Not everyone can work as a teacher. Teaching teenagers is hard!** (L56, adaptado)
