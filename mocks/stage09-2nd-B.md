## Part 1 · Yes / No quiz (20)

1. Can we cut bread with the thick edge of a knife?  **No**
2. Can you make your hands warmer by rubbing them together?  **Yes**
3. Is the Egyptian civilization one of the most ancient civilizations of the world?  **Yes**
4. Do some people sweat when they get nervous?  **Yes**
5. Is a “ten-minute-talk” a talk that lasts much more than ten minutes?  **No**
6. Would you agree to a plan that might get you into trouble with the police?  **No**
7. If something happens to you by chance, does it mean it happens unexpectedly?  **Yes**
8. Do we use the word “scarce” when we have a lot of something?  **No**
9. Do most people think something ought to be done about the poverty in the world?  **Yes**
10. Do most people feel ill at ease in strange company?  **Yes**
11. Can the weather sometimes be horrible in the middle of winter?  **Yes**
12. Is it in your own interest to wear a seat-belt in a car?  **Yes**
13. Should delicate things be handled with very little care?  **No**
14. Is a gold watch a watch that looks like gold in colour?  **No**
15. In dry countries, should people try to conserve water as much as possible?  **Yes**
16. Are birds the only animals in the world that have feathers?  **Yes**
17. Do we say hello when we greet someone in the street?  **Yes**
18. If you had left for Rio half an hour ago, would you have arrived there by now?  **No**
19. When people work for a wage, are they paid in advance?  **No**
20. Do we use towels after we take a bath?  **Yes**

## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. Factories put food in ____.  (plates / tables / **tins**)
2. I have sympathy for ____ people.  (**sick** / blessed / content)
3. Some students get very ____ before a test.  (**anxious** / anxiety / exclaim)
4. It's ____ probable that it will rain next summer.  (terribly / high / **highly**)
5. I don't understand why on ____ you didn't study for the test!  (**earth** / the sky / death)
6. Most people are afraid of the dark. I'm not afraid of it ____.  (anyway / never / **at all**)
7. Food becomes ____ in wartime.  (empty / scarcely / **scarce**)
8. A dog ____ bones in the ground.  (**buries** / burst / bucket)
9. It's not fair to judge people on first ____.  (sights / **impressions** / faces)
10. Dilma was the leader of the ____ government of my country.  (**late** / lately / later)
11. When we cut ourselves, we put a ____ of cotton wool over the cut.  (pan / pack / **pad**)
12. Students must ____ all the time during the lesson.  (**pay attention** / attention / appointment)
13. People ____ when they do exercise.  (swear / **sweat** / sweet)
14. I enjoy ____ round shops at the weekend.  (running / coming / **browsing**)
15. Lemon juice without sugar tastes ____.  (**bitter** / sweet / sour)
16. In a city like SP it´s ____ to use public transport.  (prefer / **preferable** / prefers)
17. We can use the word ____ instead of the word tidy.  (nest / **neat** / dirty)
18. There is a high level of ____ in Africa.  (poorest / **poverty** / poorer)
19. I feel ____ in strange company.  (easy / **ill at ease** / ill)
20. Eggs must be handled ____.  (**gently** / suddenly / gentle)
