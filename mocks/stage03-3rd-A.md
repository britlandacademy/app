# Simulado A · Stage 3 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

The plural of “tomato” is “tomatoes”. We cut meat with a knife. Tuesday precedes Wednesday. We can see the sun in the sky during the day. When I get up in the morning, I wash and dress. India is one of the hottest countries in the world. Most people prefer texts for short messages. The teacher says “Goodbye” to us at the end of the lesson. The moon is white, or the colour of silver. Snow is white. Clouds are made of water. My brother’s house is by the sea. We only live once. The students can enter the classroom when the teacher comes. Please remember to shake hands with Mr Jones.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What do we cut meat with?  a) a spoon  b) a knife  c) a fork → **b** (L29)
2. What do we wash our hands with?  a) a toothbrush  b) soap  c) a hat → **b** (L30)
3. What do we taste with?  a) our eyes  b) our ears  c) our tongues → **c** (L31)
4. What do we play football with?  a) a knife  b) a hat  c) a ball → **c** (L28)
5. What colour’s snow?  a) white  b) black  c) green → **a** (L34)
6. What’s the opposite of “buy”?  a) send  b) sell  c) pay → **b** (L29)
7. What are clouds made of?  a) paper  b) water  c) coal → **b** (L32)
8. What’s the opposite of deep?  a) shallow  b) heavy  c) light → **a** (L36)
9. What do we say to people on their birthday?  a) Goodbye  b) Excuse me  c) Happy Birthday → **c** (L40)
10. What’s over this building?  a) the sky  b) the floor  c) the ground → **a** (L37)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. You can carry that bag of potatoes. It isn’t heavy; it’s ____ . → **light** (L28)
2. spring, summer, ____ , winter → **autumn** (L29)
3. There’s nothing inside this box. It’s ____ . → **empty** (L30)
4. I can’t see anything in this room; it’s very ____ . Where’s the light? → **dark** (L32)
5. They go to the mosque on Tuesday and Friday. They go ____ a week. → **twice** (L34)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. My brother’s house is ____ the sea.  a) next  b) by  c) at → **b** (L28)
2. I want to go ____ holiday in the summer!  a) to  b) for  c) on → **c** (L30)
3. Can I ____ a photo of you?  a) take  b) make  c) do → **a** (L31)
4. My uncle isn’t as tall ____ my dad.  a) like  b) as  c) how → **b** (L29)
5. Nobody in my family has a car apart ____ my sister.  a) from  b) of  c) than → **a** (L32)
6. Why are all those people looking ____ us?  a) –  b) to  c) at → **c** (L33)
7. I generally ____ after I finish work.  a) give a walk  b) go for a walk  c) do walking → **b** (L34)
8. I depend ____ my parents for money because I only work two days a week.  a) on  b) of  c) – → **a** (L37)
9. Please stop ____ all my food. I didn’t buy it for you!  a) to eat  b) eating  c) that you eat → **b** (L38)
10. Why is my desk ____ the corner of the office now?  a) in  b) at  c) by → **a** (L40)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. The teacher asks each students about twenty questions. → **The teacher asks each student about twenty questions.** (L28)
2. I can’t brush my tooths because I haven’t got any toothpaste. → **I can’t brush my teeth because I haven’t got any toothpaste.** (L30)
3. Tom gets up at 8 o’clock. Ana gets up two hours early than him, at 6 o’clock. → **Tom gets up at 8 o’clock. Ana gets up two hours earlier than him, at 6 o’clock.** (L32)
4. I think you’re worst at mathematics than you are at geography. → **I think you’re worse at mathematics than you are at geography.** (L33)
5. My teacher sometimes makes me some help with answering long questions. → **My teacher sometimes gives me some help with answering long questions.** (L35)
6. It’s a quarter past two. We’re near at the end of the lesson. → **It’s a quarter past two. We’re nearly at the end of the lesson.** (L37)
7. Did you called John yesterday to ask him about our idea for the holiday? → **Did you call John yesterday to ask him about our idea for the holiday?** (L38)
8. We can only pay with card in the theatre bar. They don’t take cash. → **We can only pay by card in the theatre bar. They don’t take cash.** (L39)
9. I can play the guitar quiet well, but I don’t know how to play the piano. → **I can play the guitar quite well, but I don’t know how to play the piano.** (L41)
10. Somebody from the USA is a American. → **Somebody from the USA is an American.** (L28, adaptado)
