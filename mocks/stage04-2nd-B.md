## Part 1 · Yes / No quiz (20)

1. Is Andrômeda a broad street?  **Yes**
2. Do people generally laugh when they are unhappy?  **No**
3. Is whisky a weak drink?  **No**
4. Do we generally say “please” in English at the beginning of a request?  **No**
5. Do people generally take medicine when they are ill?  **Yes**
6. Do they sell food in clothes shops?  **No**
7. Is a tree higher than a bush?  **Yes**
8. Is it worth buying an umbrella if you live in a country where it never rains?  **No**
9. Is blood black?  **No**
10. Do people generally go to bed when they are tired?  **Yes**
11. Can we say that Afghanistan is a place like heaven?  **No**
12. Is it a good idea to book a table before going to a very popular restaurant?  **Yes**
13. Are villages noisy places to live in?  **No**
14. Do we use “shall” to ask for a suggestion?  **Yes**
15. Do tigers kill other animals for food?  **Yes**
16. Do you eat when you feel thirsty?  **No**
17. Is Estadão one of the most popular magazines in this country?  **No**
18. Are people generally sorry when they must work very hard?  **Yes**
19. Do we generally use contractions in spoken English?  **Yes**
20. Do we spell the word book: B-O-O-K?  **Yes**

## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. In some countries, men can have two or three ____.  (wifes / **wives**)
2. He ____ a lot of money in his pocket last week.  (has / **had**)
3. It's easier for me to express my ____ in speaking.  (**thoughts** / taught)
4. Did he ____ any parts of his body?  (broke / **break**)
5. You ____ an email to her last night.  (write / **wrote**)
6. The teacher ____ us a good lesson yesterday.  (**gave** / gaves)
7. I ____ to Scotland.  (**have been** / have gone)
8. I ____ here at 9am for my lesson.  (**get** / get to)
9. There ____ a table in this room last lesson.  (**was** / were)
10. I ____ tired when I don't sleep enough.  (fill / **feel**)
11. My stomach sometimes makes ____ during the night.  (**noises** / noisy)
12. Cesar Cielo is a good ____.  (**swimmer** / swimming)
13. She ____ some food at the supermarket.  (**bought** / brought)
14. Did you ____ the news on the radio last night?  (listen / **hear**)
15. I think it's ____ studying the language of another country.  (**worth** / worst)
16. People feel ill when they have ____.  (**a cold** / cold)
17. I think ____ is more pleasant for children than for adults.  (live / **life**)
18. She's ____ studying.  (tired / **tired of**)
19. My house isn't ____ building.  (in public / **a public**)
20. In my country we use black for ____.  (life / **death**)
