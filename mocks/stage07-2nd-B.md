Fonte: Exam Book, Stage 7 Exam - Test B. Parte oral (professor) fora do app. Tempo: 40 min.

## Part 1 · Yes / No quiz (20)

1. If you had a car and didn't take care of it, would it eventually stop going?  **Yes**
2. Do clouds float across the sky when it's a very windy day?  **No**
3. It is right to judge something before giving it a trial?  **No**
4. Is it right to say “I used to drive fast” if it was a habit for me in the past, but not anymore?  **Yes**
5. Can we use the verb “to bear” instead of the verb “to carry”?  **Yes**
6. Is there any difference between the words “on” and “upon”?  **No**
7. Is Bahia a southern state of Brazil?  **No**
8. To live a healthy life, does it matter what kind of food I eat?  **Yes**
9. Is a bow any use without arrows?  **No**
10. If someone hit your car and it was their fault, could you claim damages against them?  **Yes**
11. Do I gather up my things from the table at the end of the lesson?  **Yes**
12. Do we use the word “pretty” to describe a little girl or a house in the country?  **Yes**
13. When travelling, is it usually cheaper to buy two single tickets than a return ticket?  **No**
14. Is it a waste of time learning a foreign language?  **No**
15. Are children more often filled with wonder than adults?  **Yes**
16. If someone steals your umbrella, have you the right to steal someone else's?  **No**
17. Is “taste” one of the five senses?  **Yes**
18. Hadn't one better take an umbrella when it is raining?  **Yes**
19. Do we usually wave our hands to say goodbye?  **Yes**
20. If a man claimed he could jump higher than a house, would you believe him?  **No**

## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. ____ cars along the road is dangerous!  (**Racing** / Leading / Watching)
2. I only ____ John had cut his hair after he told me.  (watched / **realized** / looked)
3. The government ____ the poor village with food and water.  (gave / **supplied** / offered)
4. The Titanic ____ in 1912.  (sunk / sink / **sank**)
5. I wouldn't feel sorry for someone who said they had ____ friends.  (**a few** / few / a little)
6. I have ____ milk in my fridge. I need to buy more!  (**little** / a little / a few)
7. Getting married is a huge ____ in life!  (press / **step** / bill)
8. I don't like vegetables and I ____.  (**don't like meat either** / don't either like meat / neither don´t like meat)
9. Santa Catarina is a ____ Brazilian state.  (northern / **southern** / western)
10. Michael can play the guitar. Question: ____  (**Can Michael play the guitar?** / Michael can play the guitar? / Michael play the guitar?)
11. I ____ my clothes myself this morning.  (choose / **chose** / chosen)
12. Robin Hood was famous because of his ____ and arrows.  (sword / **bow** / shield)
13. ____ can do physical work better than an old man  (The youth / A young / **A youth**)
14. I ____ my younger sister when my parents are at work.  (**look after** / interview / look)
15. The criminal ____ the shop and was stopped by the police.  (**broke into** / went through / watched over)
16. I never ____ my time doing nothing.  (spend / earn / **waste**)
17. I only drank a ____ of wine so I could drive.  (few / **bit** / small)
18. Jack is the most ____ person I know.  (determine / determination / **determined**)
19. Bill Gates is ____ rich!  (rarely / generally / **extremely**)
20. John got irritated when his parents ____ his plans.  (**opposed** / stole / protected)
