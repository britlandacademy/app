## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (30)

1. Silk is a kind of ____.  (**fabric** / leather / clothes)
2. Hitler had a very unique ____  (son / **moustache** / body)
3. There is a large ____ of products in a supermarket.  (**range** / catalogue / building)
4. A lawyer ____ for his services.  (runs / asks permission / **charges a fee**)
5. The temperature ____ during the year.  (falls / **varies** / increases)
6. The girl ____ when she watches a sad film.  (laughs / plays / **weeps**)
7. The bride and ____ looked happy when they left the church.  (kids / **groom** / father)
8. A gun has no use without ____.  (**bullets** / criminals / batteries)
9. Homeless people usually ____.  (play indoors / **wear rags** / talk about rags)
10. The man had a ____ accent.  (**unique** / long / chemical)
11. The cake had four ____.  (options / **layers** / dollars)
12. The teenager likes to ____ younger boys by hitting them.  (be like / praise / **bully**)
13. When I record my voice, it sounds very ____.  (tall / handsome / **weird**)
14. The old man receives a ____ from the government.  (alimony / **pension** / product)
15. The heart is one of the ____ organs of the body.  (**vital** / fattest / ugliest)
16. The girl was ____ with a knife and died.  (threatened / killed / **stabbed**)
17. The comedy film was ____.  (sad / **hilarious** / difficult)
18. The wedding ceremony was ____. Everybody loved it.  (**amazing** / boring / ridiculous)
19. There is some orange juice inside the glass ____.  (cabinet / **jug** / fridge)
20. The swimmer ____ for air after being under water for a long time.  (cried / helped / **gasped**)
21. The phrasal verb give up means to ____.  (**stop a habit** / get to the same point as somebody else / distribute to a group of people)
22. The phrasal verb get along with means to ____.  (**have a good relationship with someone** / cancel something / arrive at a destination)
23. The phrasal verb set up means to ____.  (**start an organization** / come without an appointment / postpone something)
24. The phrasal verb get the sack means to ____.  (end a relationship / test something / **get fired**)
25. The phrasal verb pay o means to ____.  (**repay money that is owed** / to tolerate or accept something / hit someone with a vehicle)
26. The phrasal verb show o means to ____.  (leave home for a social event / **act extra special for people watching** / check or examine)
27. The phrasal verb come down means to ____  (have someone over / visit a person or a place / **move to a lower level**)
28. The phrasal verb break even means to ____.  (have changes in the weather / **keep costs and profits even** / respect someone)
29. The phrasal verb pick on means to ____.  (**treat somebody unfairly** / display things / begin a journey)
30. The phrasal verb carry out means to ____.  (**follow and complete a task** / leave a station / examine something)
