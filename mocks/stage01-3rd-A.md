# Simulado A · Stage 1 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), Yes/No (15), múltipla escolha (12), correção de erros (8) = 50 pontos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item (“novo” = item novo, no máximo 10%).

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

The pen is long. The pencil is short. The book is on the table. The clock is in front of you. I am standing on the floor. Anna is a girl. Jack is a boy. The window is open. The door is closed. There are three books on the table. This pencil is red. That chair is black. They are sitting in the room. We are standing in front of the house. The wall is high. Mr Brown is tall. I can touch my nose. I can’t touch the ceiling. I like my city. She speaks English. He goes home after the lesson. Touch your head, please. Open your book. Where’s the teacher?

## Part 2 · Yes / No quiz (15), lido em voz alta duas vezes

## Part 3 · Multiple choice (12)

1. I ____ John.  a) am  b) is  c) are → **a**
2. Anna isn’t a woman; she’s a ____ .  a) man  b) boy  c) girl → **c**
3. I’m taking the pen ____ the table.  a) of  b) from  c) in → **b**
4. Jack: “____ pencil’s yellow?” Anna: “This pencil’s yellow.”  a) Which  b) What  c) Who → **a**
5. The letter L is ____ M and N.  a) before  b) after  c) between → **c**
6. ____ are three books on that table.  a) There  b) This  c) They → **a**
7. I’m ____ to the window.  a) reading  b) writing  c) going → **c**
8. Jack’s shoes are on his ____ .  a) legs  b) feet  c) thumbs → **b**
9. Please translate these English words ____ French.  a) with  b) for  c) into → **c**
10. There aren’t ____ coats in the room.  a) any  b) some  c) none → **a**
11. The students are going ____ .  a) home  b) to home  c) at home → **a**
12. I can’t ____ the ceiling.  a) to touch  b) touching  c) touch → **c**

## Part 4 · Error correction (8)

1. Is he going to home? → **Is he going home?** (L11)
2. She isn’t speaking French; is speaking English. → **She isn’t speaking French; she is speaking English.** (L11)
3. What’s the difference Beijing and China? → **What’s the difference between Beijing and China?** (L9)
4. How many clock are there in this room? → **How many clocks are there in this room?** (L6)
5. Mr and Mrs Brown has a large house. → **Mr and Mrs Brown have a large house.** (L10)
6. I can’t to touch the ceiling. → **I can’t touch the ceiling.** (L12)
7. Walter speak German with his family. → **Walter speaks German with his family.** (L11)
8. Is these his shoes? → **Are these his shoes?** (L7)
