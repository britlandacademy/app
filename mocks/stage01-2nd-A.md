## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. The box is ____.  (**small** / large)
2. She isn´t a girl, she´s a ____.  (man / **woman**)
3. The house is behind ____.  (he / **him**)
4. I ____ closing the book.  (is / **am**)
5. You ____ reading the book.  (isn´t / **aren´t**)
6. He´s ____ the pen on the floor.  (**putting** / taking)
7. The light is on the ____.  (table / **ceiling**)
8. He isn´t Mr. Brown, he´s ____ Brown.  (Anna / **Jack**)
9. I´m standing on the ____.  (room / **floor**)
10. What ____ doing?  (**am I** / I am)
11. They ____ opening the window.  (is / **are**)
12. I´m putting my shoes on my ____.  (foots / **feet**)
13. ____ pictures are there on these walls?  (**How many** / How much)
14. “E” is the ____ letter of the alphabet.  (**fifth** / fifty)
15. This pencil is ____ in front of me.  (there / **here**)
16. We ____ sitting on the floor.  (isn´t / **aren´t**)
17. The plural of woman is ____.  (**women** / womans)
18. The letter “H” isn´t a ____.  (consonant / **vowel**)
19. The letter “I” is ____ the letter B.  (**after** / before)
20. The house is in front of ____.  (**them** / they)
