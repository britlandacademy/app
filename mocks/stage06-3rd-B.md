# Simulado B · Stage 6 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

If someone hit a policeman, they’d be arrested. Another word for midday is noon. Two other words for “ill” are “sick” and “unwell”. We can use rope for climbing mountains. The opposites of “overcook” and “overpay” are “undercook” and “underpay”. Mud is a mixture of earth and water. Another name for a chemist’s is a pharmacy. A thousand grams make a kilogram. An island is a piece of land completely surrounded by water. A fortnight consists of fourteen days. We call the fatty part of fresh milk the cream. Brian stood up, tried to walk to the door, and immediately fell over!

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What are the three forms of “choose”?  a) choose, chose, chosen  b) choose, choosed, choosed  c) choose, chose, chose → **a** (L84)
2. What’s the past of “ride”?  a) ridden  b) rode  c) rided → **b** (L86)
3. What’s the past of “fall”?  a) falled  b) fallen  c) fell → **c** (L88)
4. What are the three forms of “blow”?  a) blow, blew, blown  b) blow, blowed, blowed  c) blow, blew, blew → **a** (L89)
5. What kind of matter are books made of?  a) glass  b) paper  c) water → **b** (L95)
6. Which modal is the only exception to this rule?  a) “can”  b) “would”  c) “ought” → **c** (L98)
7. What do we call the words “who”, “which” and “that”?  a) relative pronouns  b) modal verbs  c) indirect speech → **a** (L100)
8. What are the three forms of “fly”?  a) fly, flied, flied  b) fly, flew, flown  c) fly, flew, flew → **b** (L93)
9. What are the three forms of “fight”?  a) fight, fighted, fighted  b) fight, fought, fighten  c) fight, fought, fought → **c** (L87)
10. What does the expression “every now and again” mean?  a) sometimes but not often  b) every day  c) never → **a** (L96)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. You can either ____ in this form now and hand it back to me, or you can do everything online at home if you prefer. → **fill** (L87)
2. As many people are choosing not to have children nowadays, the ____ of my country has decreased since the start of this century. → **population** (L88)
3. They need to ____ this street. It’s so narrow. It’s almost impossible to pass a car coming the other way. → **widen** (L91)
4. If we want to eat in this restaurant, we’ll have to stand in the ____ , and it’s really long. Can’t we go somewhere else? → **queue** (L97)
5. On the shortest day of the year in London, sunrise is at 8:04 and ____ is at 15:53. → **sunset** (L99)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. Solomon encouraged his sons ____ to the industrial north of the country to find work.  a) to move  b) for moving  c) they should move → **a** (L83)
2. I have no idea ____ Charles knew where all the money was, but he knew.  a) supposing  b) whether  c) how → **b** (L83)
3. Which do you consider ____ the most important: health, money or love?  a) being  b) as it is  c) to be → **c** (L89)
4. The decision to drive Michael to the hospital was an easy one to ____ ; he looked terrible!  a) do  b) choose  c) make → **c** (L89)
5. Excuse me. Are you ____ charge of those children? They’re running around the restaurant and making a terrible noise.  a) in  b) with  c) over → **a** (L93)
6. Victor told me he’d be late, but I expect ____ pretty soon.  a) him to come  b) him coming  c) that he comes → **a** (L95)
7. The children ____ turns to see who could say the English alphabet the fastest!  a) played  b) took  c) held → **b** (L96)
8. We got to the station ____ time to have a coffee before walking to the platform for our train.  a) by the  b) on  c) in → **c** (L98)
9. Eduardo has offered me £100 ____ exchange for helping him to move house.  a) by  b) in  c) with → **b** (L100)
10. I’m sorry but this plan doesn’t ____ sense. We need to go away and think about it more.  a) look like  b) make much  c) provide any → **b** (L101)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. I wouldn’t say my ankle was painful, but it feels rather oddly when I start running. → **I wouldn’t say my ankle was painful, but it feels rather odd when I start running.** (L86)
2. I don’t know which staff member was last to leave the store yesterday, but who it was failed to lock the main door. → **I don’t know which staff member was last to leave the store yesterday, but whoever it was failed to lock the main door.** (L90)
3. The sea was rough yesterday, and a few passengers were seasick. However, the sea’s very smooth now, so I don’t think we’ll have the same problem today. → **The sea was rough yesterday, and a few passengers were seasick. However, the sea’s very calm now, so I don’t think we’ll have the same problem today.** (L92)
4. If you really don’t want to visit Uncle Fred, than you don’t have to come with us. You can stay here with your sister if you prefer. → **If you really don’t want to visit Uncle Fred, then you don’t have to come with us. You can stay here with your sister if you prefer.** (L94)
5. I can’t wait till all these exams are over. They get so stressing! → **I can’t wait till all these exams are over. They get so stressful!** (L98)
6. Of course I can roast a chicken. You should have more faith by my cooking skills! → **Of course I can roast a chicken. You should have more faith in my cooking skills!** (L84, adaptado)
7. When’s your boyfriend arriving? I can’t wait for meeting him! → **When’s your boyfriend arriving? I can’t wait to meet him!** (L85, adaptado)
8. Might Paula use to live in Spain before she moved to England? She speaks the language so well. → **Did Paula use to live in Spain before she moved to England? She speaks the language so well.** (L86, adaptado)
9. Agata had spent about ten minutes trying to open the ketchup bottle but couldn’t. → **Agata spent about ten minutes trying to open the ketchup bottle but couldn’t.** (L94, adaptado)
10. This summer holiday, you’re happy to visit somewhere you’ve been to before, don’t you? → **This summer holiday, you’re happy to visit somewhere you’ve been to before, aren’t you?** (L95, adaptado)
