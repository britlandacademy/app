# Simulado B · Stage 10 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

We use a ladder for climbing up walls and trees. A saddle is a seat on a horse, bicycle or motorbike. Meat rots more quickly in the heat. We use wax for making candles. We say “Bless you!” when someone sneezes. A cereal box is usually made of cardboard. A lion makes a roaring noise. We use a spade for digging holes in the earth. The Crown Jewels are kept in the Tower of London. The cow is sacred in India. Doors are normally rectangular in shape. If we had a spare room, you’d be welcome to stay at our place, but we don’t.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What do we mean by an earnest person?  a) someone who’s serious and sincere  b) someone who’s funny  c) someone who’s lazy → **a** (L162)
2. What do we call the sides of a river?  a) the roads  b) the banks  c) the bridges → **b** (L164)
3. What are railway tracks?  a) the lines on which trains run  b) the doors of a train  c) the seats on a train → **a** (L159)
4. What are the three forms of “spin”?  a) spin, spinned, spinned  b) spin, spun, spun  c) spin, span, spin → **b** (L165)
5. What are the three forms of “weave”?  a) weave, waved, waved  b) weave, weaved, weaved  c) weave, wove, woven → **c** (L166)
6. What kind of things can we make by weaving?  a) cloth, baskets etc.  b) cars and planes  c) houses made of glass → **a** (L166)
7. What does the verb “account for” mean?  a) to count money  b) to give a reason or explanation for  c) to be angry → **b** (L168)
8. What are the three forms of “bleed”?  a) bleed, bled, bled  b) bleed, bleeded, bleeded  c) bleed, blood, blood → **a** (L169)
9. What kind of work does a mechanic do?  a) bakes bread  b) cuts hair  c) repairs cars and other machines → **c** (L171)
10. What do young children call adults?  a) teachers  b) grown-ups  c) doctors → **b** (L172)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. I sang this song at a school concert once. I still know all the words by ____ . → **heart** (L158)
2. We couldn’t see the bride’s face. She hadn’t yet lifted her ____ . → **veil** (L162)
3. Excuse me. I have to be in London as soon as possible. Could you tell me the ____ time for the next train? → **departure** (L165)
4. We just crossed the ____ between England and Scotland, and you didn’t even notice. → **border** (L166)
5. I believed he was innocent, but the twelve members of the ____ thought otherwise and found him guilty. → **jury** (L171)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. The street noise outside our window isn’t something I’m willing to put up ____ . I’m going to ask for a new room.  a) with  b) by  c) on → **a** (L158)
2. Our chemistry teacher disapproved ____ the experiment we did.  a) for  b) of  c) on → **b** (L159)
3. It’s not looking great outside, but we should be OK so ____ as we’ve got umbrellas.  a) far  b) long  c) much → **b** (L161)
4. I told you about my plan ____ confidence, so why does Robert know all about it?!  a) as  b) in  c) with → **b** (L162)
5. Greg was the best player in our team ____ far. He could have become a professional.  a) at  b) for  c) by → **c** (L163)
6. Matt attempted ____ through the window, but they pulled him back inside.  a) he’d escape  b) escaping  c) to escape → **c** (L167)
7. If we ____ , I’d probably still be living with my family in Italy.  a) wouldn’t get married  b) never met each other  c) hadn’t fallen in love → **c** (L168)
8. This food is great! How long is it since ____ in a restaurant together? Ages, isn’t it?!  a) we ate  b) we’ve eaten  c) we’re eating → **a** (L169)
9. Desmond is a strange boss. Sometimes, it’s impossible to work ____ what he expects you to do.  a) onto  b) around  c) out → **c** (L169)
10. As your doctor, Mrs White, I recommend you cut down ____ coffee. Eight cups a day is too many.  a) for  b) on  c) from → **b** (L174)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. The police had no evidence at all that we’d committed any crime. They arrested us for suspicion, just because we ran away when we saw the car. → **The police had no evidence at all that we’d committed any crime. They arrested us on suspicion, just because we ran away when we saw the car.** (L174)
2. My new office chair fell off pieces within a couple of days of use. → **My new office chair fell to pieces within a couple of days of use.** (L160, adaptado)
3. If you’re running down of money, I could lend you some. → **If you’re running short of money, I could lend you some.** (L162, adaptado)
4. Words communicate a lot. Anyway, more information is often communicated by how the words are said. → **Words communicate a lot. However, more information is often communicated by how the words are said.** (L165, adaptado)
5. Nobody can account to the strange patterns in the wheat field, so they were probably made by aliens. → **Nobody can account for the strange patterns in the wheat field, so they were probably made by aliens.** (L168, adaptado)
6. The town isn’t dangerous, but we’d recommend leaving valuable possessions in the hotel, just to go on the safe side. → **The town isn’t dangerous, but we’d recommend leaving valuable possessions in the hotel, just to be on the safe side.** (L169, adaptado)
7. Vanessa’s face went completely red when I accused her for eating the biscuits. → **Vanessa’s face went completely red when I accused her of eating the biscuits.** (L172, adaptado)
8. Maybe this was an unfortunate accident. Maybe nobody’s at blame. → **Maybe this was an unfortunate accident. Maybe nobody’s to blame.** (L164, adaptado)
9. Sue’s somewhat alike to her sister, both physically and in her character. → **Sue’s somewhat similar to her sister, both physically and in her character.** (L159, adaptado)
10. I’d never discourage my daughter joining the army if she wanted to. → **I’d never discourage my daughter from joining the army if she wanted to.** (L173, adaptado)
