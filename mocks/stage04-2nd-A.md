3. Do most people die when they are young?  **No**
4. Are boys generally physically stronger than men?  **No**
5. Do most people have two feet?  **Yes**
6. Do you eat when you feel thirsty?  **No**
7. Is smoking bad for the health?  **Yes**
8. Do you bring whisky with you to school?  **No**
9. Do young children get their parents from school?  **No**
10. Will it rain next year?  **No**
11. Is your pencil worth as much now as it was when you bought it?  **No**
12. Do doctors generally suggest that people stop smoking?  **Yes**

## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. Did you like the ____ of Cinderella when you were a child?  (**story** / history)
2. I ____ English during the last lesson.  (**spoke** / speak)
3. We ____ a lot of wine last night.  (drunk / **drank**)
4. He ____ on that chair during the last lesson.  (**sat** / saw)
5. Did you ____ anything last lesson?  (**write** / wrote)
6. She ____ a car yesterday.  (**drove** / droves)
7. When I am ____ I eat.  (**hungry** / angry)
8. I ____ school at 9am for my lesson.  (get / **get to**)
9. There ____ four pictures on the wall last lesson.  (was / **were**)
10. I ____ my stomach completely when I eat.  (**fill** / feel)
11. I don't know ____ I'll go to Ubatuba during the weekend or not  (if / **whether**)
12. If you study hard, you'll ____ learning English well.  (succeed / **succeed in**)
13. He ____ his books to the lesson yesterday.  (bought / **brought**)
14. I'll keep ____ English until I die!  (**studying** / study)
15. It's sometimes difficult to understand ____ English.  (spoke / **spoken**)
16. I ____ to study for my exam next week, because I studied last night.  (mustn't / **don't have**)
17. I think it's worth ____ the language of another country.  (study / **studying**)
18. We ____ go home after the lesson.  (**will** / ill)
19. My house isn't ____ building.  (in public / **a public**)
20. Michael Jackson ____ a few years ago.  (**died** / die)
