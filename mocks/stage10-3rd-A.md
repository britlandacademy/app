# Simulado A · Stage 10 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

By an earnest person, we mean someone who’s serious and sincere. We call the sides of a river the banks. Railway tracks are the lines on which trains run. The three forms of “spin” are “spin, spun, spun”. The three forms of “weave” are “weave, wove, woven”. We can make cloth and baskets by weaving. The verb “account for” means “give a reason or explanation for”. The three forms of “bleed” are “bleed, bled, bled”. A mechanic repairs cars and other machines. Young children call adults grown-ups. We are now approaching London Kings Cross. Did you see that flash of lightning? The blade on this knife needs to be kept sharp.

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

6. Words communicate a lot. ____ , more information is often communicated by how the words are said.  a) Anyway  b) Besides  c) However → **c** (L165)
7. Nobody can account ____ the strange patterns in the wheat field, so they were probably made by aliens.  a) for  b) to  c) of → **a** (L168)

10. I’d never discourage my daughter ____ the army if she wanted to.  a) from joining  b) to join  c) that she joined → **a** (L173)
