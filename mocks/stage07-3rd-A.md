# Simulado A · Stage 7 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

The three forms of “lead” are “lead, led, led”. The three forms of “sink” are “sink, sank, sunk”. The three forms of “lay” are “lay, laid, laid”. The three forms of “steal” are “steal, stole, stolen”. The United Kingdom includes England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. The five senses are sight, hearing, taste, touch and smell. The word “unless” means “except if”. The only safe time to walk along this beach is at low tide. If you want to go abroad, you’ll probably need your passport. Derek took the bill from the waiter and paid it immediately. Cats have a better sense of hearing than dogs. There’s steam coming out of the kettle.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What does the expression “quite a few” mean?  a) a fairly large number of  b) none  c) very few → **a** (L106)
2. What’s a snag?  a) a kind of bag  b) a small problem  c) a new road → **b** (L111)
3. What’s a seat?  a) anything that we eat  b) anything that we wear  c) anything that we sit on → **c** (L113)
4. What do we mean by a dull person?  a) a boring person  b) a clever person  c) a happy person → **a** (L113)
5. What do the words “or so” mean?  a) never  b) exactly  c) about → **c** (L119)
6. How much is 3 times 10?  a) thirty  b) thirteen  c) three hundred → **a** (L119)
7. What do we do with waste paper?  a) we eat it  b) we recycle it  c) we drink it → **b** (L111)
8. How much is 12 divided by 4?  a) three  b) four  c) eight → **a** (L119)
9. What are the three forms of “light”?  a) light, lighted, lighted  b) light, lit, lighten  c) light, lit, lit → **c** (L111)
10. What are the three forms of “quit”?  a) quit, quit, quit  b) quit, quitted, quitted  c) quit, quet, quit → **a** (L119)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. My daughter’s daughter’s my ____ . → **granddaughter** (L102)
2. If he takes even one more ____ towards us, arrest him. → **step** (L104)
3. My girlfriend and I have very similar ____ in music; we both love rock. → **tastes** (L106)
4. I’m so sorry you had to go ____ an experience like that. Are you ok now? → **through** (L107)
5. Excellent news! It looks ____ if the old post office at the end of the street is going to become a restaurant. → **as** (L111)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. Marisa shouldn’t ____ to her father like that yesterday; it was rude.  a) spoken  b) be spoken  c) have spoken → **c** (L102)
2. I wanted to get a car but didn’t buy anything ____ the end. I don’t really need one.  a) at  b) in  c) by → **b** (L103)
3. Bob hates the food here in Spain, and he doesn’t like the weather ____ .  a) either  b) too  c) as well → **a** (L104)
4. What do you mean you’re not looking forward ____ your wedding?!  a) at  b) for  c) to → **c** (L105)
5. I’m afraid I can’t talk to you right ____ . Can I call you back in five minutes?  a) moment  b) now  c) at present → **b** (L106)
6. I’m not sure that hotel was ____ the money we paid. And the manager was useless!  a) value  b) charging  c) worth → **c** (L108)
7. That was a long run. You ____ be tired now!  a) can  b) must  c) would → **b** (L109)
8. My husband doesn’t drive, and ____ .  a) neither do I  b) so don’t I  c) nor I don’t → **a** (L110)
9. Would you mind ____ while I’m talking, please?  a) to be quiet  b) if you’re quiet  c) being quiet → **c** (L112)
10. I wish we ____ more about this neighbourhood before moving here.  a) had known  b) knew  c) were knowing → **a** (L116)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. Did you use to get nervous at school when teachers asked you to read out loudly in front of the rest of the class? → **Did you use to get nervous at school when teachers asked you to read out loud in front of the rest of the class?** (L102)
2. The shop made me an offer of £650 for my guitar, which I accepted because it was much too low. I decided not to sell the instrument. → **The shop made me an offer of £650 for my guitar, which I rejected because it was much too low. I decided not to sell the instrument.** (L105)
3. You can confess everything to us now if you want, but you also have the right to ask a lawer for advice first. It’s your decision. → **You can confess everything to us now if you want, but you also have the right to ask a lawyer for advice first. It’s your decision.** (L107)
4. That pullover’s never going to keep you warmly on the farm all day; you’ll need a jacket too, won’t you? → **That pullover’s never going to keep you warm on the farm all day; you’ll need a jacket too, won’t you?** (L108)
5. I’m going to marry Lisa; who is, if she says yes! → **I’m going to marry Lisa; that is, if she says yes!** (L109, adaptado)
6. A strong coffee each morning is fine, but it is not wise by drinking it throughout the day as if it were water. → **A strong coffee each morning is fine, but it is not wise to drink it throughout the day as if it were water.** (L112, adaptado)
7. We’d better inform the police about the gun we found in the garden, wouldn’t we? → **We’d better inform the police about the gun we found in the garden, hadn’t we?** (L114, adaptado)
8. I commanded the men they should enter the building. → **I commanded the men to enter the building.** (L117, adaptado)
9. Is it true that eating oranges helps to guard from colds? → **Is it true that eating oranges helps to guard against colds?** (L118, adaptado)
10. Do you think telling jokes is a good way to clear the ice at dinner parties? → **Do you think telling jokes is a good way to break the ice at dinner parties?** (L119, adaptado)
