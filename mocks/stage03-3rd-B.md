# Simulado B · Stage 3 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

We play football with a ball. We taste with our tongues. We brush our teeth with a toothbrush. I can’t see anything in this room because it’s very dark. A millionaire is a person who has a million pounds or more. Our hair covers our heads. The ceiling is over our heads. Last season lasted about three months. The plural of “sheep” is “sheep”. Coal is black. We spoke English during the last lesson. Sheep are heavier animals than chickens. I want to pay for these drinks. Please remember to shake hands with Mr Jones when he arrives. We go to the mosque twice a week.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What nationality is somebody from the USA?  a) American  b) English  c) Spanish → **a** (L28)
2. What can we see in the sky during the day?  a) the moon  b) the sun  c) a ceiling → **b** (L29)
3. What’s the plural of “tooth”?  a) tooths  b) tooth’s  c) teeth → **c** (L30)
4. Which is one of the coldest countries in the world?  a) India  b) Finland  c) Brazil → **b** (L31)
5. What covers our heads?  a) our hands  b) our hair  c) our feet → **b** (L34)
6. What did you use to answer the last question?  a) my hands  b) my feet  c) my voice → **c** (L38)
7. What’s the opposite of the word “quick”?  a) slow  b) fast  c) late → **a** (L34)
8. What’s the past tense of the verb “do”?  a) does  b) did  c) doing → **b** (L38)
9. How many ears have you got?  a) one  b) two  c) three → **b** (L39)
10. What colour’s coal?  a) white  b) grey  c) black → **c** (L42)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. In winter, I wear ____ on my hands. → **gloves** (L30)
2. January follows December, and November ____ it. → **precedes** (L29)
3. We say that the sun is not shining when there are a lot of ____ in the sky. → **clouds** (L32)
4. This river isn’t deep; it’s very ____ . You can walk from one side to the other. → **shallow** (L36)
5. I want to pay for these drinks. You ____ for the last ones. → **paid** (L42)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. I generally leave ____ house at 8 o’clock in the morning.  a) my  b) –  c) from my → **a** (L29)
2. I receive many emails each week, but I don’t reply ____ many of them.  a) for  b) –  c) to → **c** (L31)
3. Both my children are very good at ____ tennis.  a) they play  b) playing  c) play → **b** (L33)
4. Alice was in the class last lesson and the lesson ____ that.  a) before  b) ago  c) earlier → **a** (L35)
5. Please ____ if you want me to work tomorrow.  a) message me  b) text to me  c) make me contact → **a** (L31)
6. We’re going to Paris ____ our brother.  a) because we see  b) for seeing  c) to see → **c** (L33)
7. Please remember ____ hands with Mr Jones when he arrives.  a) to shake  b) shaking  c) shake → **a** (L37)
8. We ____ go to the theatre. We prefer it to the cinema, and it’s very close to our flat.  a) rarely  b) often  c) nearly → **b** (L38)
9. Is it a ____ way from here to Scotland?  a) large  b) far  c) long → **c** (L39)
10. Angela: “Hi, Leo. How’s it ____ ? Is everything OK with you?”  a) making  b) doing  c) going → **c** (L41)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. I don’t think Magda speaks with anybody in the class other that me. → **I don’t think Magda speaks with anybody in the class other than me.** (L34)
2. I’m always sad in this city because every the buildings are grey and the sun doesn’t shine much. → **I’m always sad in this city because all the buildings are grey and the sun doesn’t shine much.** (L35)
3. I can understand English well when I read, but I go to a Callan Method school because I want to talk it well too. → **I can understand English well when I read, but I go to a Callan Method school because I want to speak it well too.** (L38)
4. Why did you spoke to your cousin like that? It was very impolite. → **Why did you speak to your cousin like that? It was very impolite.** (L42)
5. We wash our hands with a soap. → **We wash our hands with soap.** (L30, adaptado)
6. The plural of “sheep” is “sheeps”. → **The plural of “sheep” is “sheep”.** (L39, adaptado)
7. I’m agree with you. → **I agree with you.** (L40, adaptado)
8. Nobody’s sitting beside to you. → **Nobody’s sitting beside you.** (L34, adaptado)
9. When the teacher comes, the students can enter into the classroom. → **When the teacher comes, the students can enter the classroom.** (L34)
10. The ceiling’s over of our heads. → **The ceiling’s over our heads.** (L37, adaptado)
