# Stage 7 · Mock A · 2nd edition (Exam Book, Test A)

Fonte: Exam Book, Stage 7 Exam - Test A. Parte oral (professor) fora do app. Tempo: 40 min.

## Part 1 · Yes / No quiz (20)

1. Would a piece of iron sink if you put it onto water?  **Yes**
2. Is low tide when the water comes into the land?  **No**
3. If I say that I have “a few books”, does it mean that I haven't got enough books?  **No**
4. Is it stupid to judge something before we give it a trial?  **Yes**
5. Are “at last” and “lastly” completely different from each other?  **Yes**
6. Do people generally say: “I walked very far yesterday”?  **No**
7. Can we consider studying as a waste of time?  **No**
8. Does the idiom “fed up” mean bored and tired?  **Yes**
9. Is a determined person someone who stops doing things in the middle?  **No**
10. Can people go to prison if they are caught stealing?  **Yes**
11. Is hearing one of the five senses?  **Yes**
12. Can the police solve a crime with the help of clues?  **Yes**
13. Do countries like Canada and Switzerland suffer from lack of food?  **No**
14. Is it easier to grow food on a mountain than on a plain?  **No**
15. Must we go to a lawyer or to the law courts to demand our rights?  **Yes**
16. Do “racehorse” and horserace” mean the same thing?  **No**
17. Is a teacup a cup used for tea?  **Yes**
18. Does “to be tidy” mean to keep things in their proper place?  **Yes**
19. Do most birds lay their eggs about once a year?  **Yes**
20. Could you bear the weight of a car on your back?  **No**

## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. Superman lived a life full of ____.  (kindness / leadership / **adventure**)
2. The boy received many ____ on his birthday.  (**gifts** / snags / barrels)
3. My car's ____ broke down and I had to stop in the middle of the road.  (**engine** / seat / pipe)
4. The lion is a ____ animal.  (dull / **fierce** / kind)
5. The strange man ____ the shop and stole everything.  (**broke into** / looked after / took care)
6. It ____ me when people eat with their mouths open.  (surprises / pleases / **irritates**)
7. I sometimes ____ my money on useless things.  (pay / draw / **waste**)
8. I can't speak Chinese and ____.  (so can he / **neither can he** / neither can't he)
9. Anna can't ____ in her old clothes. She's an adult now!  (**fit** / gather / determine)
10. There's a serious ____ of food in poor countries.  (frequency / **lack** / supply)
11. The bird broke its ____ and couldn't fly.  (nose / **wing** / bow)
12. Children's rooms are rarely ____ when they are playing.  (deal / settle / **tidy**)
13. During the football game the player ____ himself and couldn't play any longer.  (**injured** / wounded / damaged)
14. The shop owner couldn't ____ the person who stole from him.  (identity / **identify** / run)
15. Gisele Bundchen is popular for her ____.  (greed / **beauty** / intelligence)
16. The boat eventually ____ because of the hole in it.  (**sank** / drowned / sunk)
17. An airplane must be ____ expensive!  (a bit / **extremely** / entirely)
18. There's a large quantity of wine in that ____.  (bow / bill / **barrel**)
19. I'll teach my children about the ____ of being honest.  (determination / **importance** / success)
20. Sabesp ____ São José dos Campos with water.  (leads / **supplies** / lends)
