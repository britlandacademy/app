## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. I'm ____ English.  (**speaking** / speak)
2. You don't ____ the book during the lesson.  (**read** / reading)
3. ____ going home?  (**Are you** / You are)
4. She ____ in English.  (**writes** / write)
5. Does he ____ that picture?  (**like** / likes)
6. I ____ to the door after the lesson.  (**go** / going)
7. He ____ speak Portuguese during the lesson.  (don't / **doesn't**)
8. There are ____ people in a town than in a village.  (**more** / fewer)
9. 25 is a ____ of a hundred.  (**quarter** / half)
10. Both of ____ speak English.  (they / **them**)
11. Chinese is an ____ language.  (European / **Asian**)
12. My eyes are a different colour ____ your eyes.  (**from** / as)
13. There isn't ____ in this room wearing a hat.  (**anybody** / nobody)
14. I ____ after the lesson.  (**stand up** / standing up)
15. It's ____ for me to speak my language.  (difficult / **easy**)
16. Mr. Brown is ____.  (**married** / single)
17. His mother's husband's name is ____.  (**John** / Mary)
18. Santa Catarina is in the ____ of Brazil.  (North / **South**)
19. I can write ____ a pen or a pencil.  (**with** / without)
20. Not ____ the people in this town are women. .  (wall / **all**)
