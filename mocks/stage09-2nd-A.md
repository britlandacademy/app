5. Does tea with sugar taste bitter?  **No**
6. Do people generally rub their hands together to make them warmer in summer time?  **No**

9. Is a peculiar sound a sound that may be difficult to identify?  **Yes**

## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. Her car is her most valuable personal ____.  (possessed / possessive / **possession**)
2. I would love to ____ Bill Gates at my house!  (**entertain** / entertaining / entertainer)
3. My friend needs to ____ for a new passport.  (**apply** / applied / applies)
4. It´s ____ probable that it´ll rain later.  (high / higher / **highly**)
5. I would never ____ a plan that would put me in prison.  (agree about / agree that / **agree to**)
6. We use a ____ to remove pencil marks.  (**rubber** / rub / rub out)
7. He has ____ any money.  (scarce / scarf / **scarcely**)
8. It ____ me when I see any kind of injustice.  (angry / **angers** / hunger)
9. I was ____ the dark when I was a child.  (afraid that / afraid to / **afraid of**)
10. A cat uses its whiskers to measure the width of a gap in a ____.  (edge / **hedge** / bridge)
11. Most women like to ____ their handbags with their shoes.  (catch / **match** / path)
12. The “Real” is the main unit of ____ in my country.  (current / currently / **currency**)
13. Before buying a car, it´s better to ____ at the engine first.  (**take a look** / take a hold of / take a chance)
14. Every now and again we get the ____ to do something a bit dishonest.  (tempt / **temptation** / tempting)
15. Pinocchio is a famous ____.  (lie / lay / **liar**)
16. People ____ a lot during physical exercise.  (**sweat** / sweet / swear)
17. Sometimes I ____ what´s the purpose of living.  (wander / wonderful / **wonder**)
18. I think the internet is the most important ____ in history.  (invent / invented / **invention**)
19. I need a detailed ____ of what you´ve been doing here, please.  (explain / explained / **explanation**)
20. We can use ____ instead of neat.  (**tidy** / tide / tight)
