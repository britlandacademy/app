1. Is it more comfortable to sleep lying down than sitting up?  **Yes**
2. Is Michael Jackson alive today?  **No**
3. Do most people feel alive when they have unpleasant work to do?  **No**
4. If you dropped from the top of a very high building, would you die?  **Yes**
5. Has this exam finished yet?  **No**
6. Should students study for exams?  **Yes**
7. If there weren't any food in the world, would we all live?  **No**
8. Is learning a new language usually very useful to most people?  **Yes**
9. If a flower had no water, would it die?  **Yes**
10. If today were Thursday, would tomorrow be Wednesday?  **No**
11. Can a mixture of tea, coffee and onion make a pleasant drink?  **No**
12. If you were late for an important appointment, would you take your time?  **No**
13. Is 'little water' the opposite of 'plenty of water'?  **Yes**
14. Have you been in this room for more than three hours?  **No**
15. Is São Paulo an agricultural city?  **No**
16. Were we living a thousand years ago?  **No**
17. Is the sentence 'The bread is cut by me' in the passive voice?  **Yes**
18. Are the streets wet when it rains?  **Yes**
19. Is the pronoun 'himself' called a reflexive pronoun?  **Yes**
20. Do footballers have numbers written on their backs?  **Yes**

## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. People ____ bad if they eat too much.  (**feel** / fill / few)
2. He ____ his stomach completely when he eats.  (feels / **fills** / feel)
3. You ____ goodbye to us last lesson.  (say / **said** / says)
4. Every day she has to ____ to catch an early train.  (take her time / apologize / **hurry**)
5. She doesn't ____ them any money.  (**owe** / own / owes)
6. It's worth ____ a little more, and going to a good hairdresser.  (**paying** / earning / buying)
7. The first thing she saw when she ____ was the ceiling.  (wake up / **woke up** / woken up)
8. They didn't do anything ____ last month.  (use / usually / **unusual**)
9. Winter hasn't finished ____.  (still / now / **yet**)
10. I wasn't ____ the dark when I was a child.  (afraid / **afraid of** / afraid to)
11. She has never ____ the lottery.  (lost / found / **won**)
12. The best way to ____ against becoming ill is to exercise.  (**guard** / on your guard / o your guard)
13. The soldier ____ by surprise without his sword.  (**was taken** / taking / were taken)
14. If a student makes a mistake in his dictation, it's usually his ____  (faulty / **fault** / faultless)
15. My mother ____ me when I was a child.  (cares for / care / **cared for**)
16. The opposite of plenty of water is ____.  (much water / more water / **little water**)
17. You can ____ down if you want to touch your feet.  (**bend** / avoid / wait)
18. Sophie wouldn't like to be a famous ____.  (act / actor / **actress**)
19. He ____ you a pen and you never returned it.  (lended / **lent** / lend)
20. You shouldn't ____ if you don't understand some questions.  (worried / be worry / **worry**)
