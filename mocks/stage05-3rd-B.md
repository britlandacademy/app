# Simulado B · Stage 5 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

The flight only took two hours. The word “mad” means angry, very interested, and crazy. We can use the expression “If I were you” to give advice. Three feet make a yard. The English alphabet is composed of twenty-six letters. The word “towards” means “in the direction of”. The past of “fly” is “flew”. Footballers’ shirts have names and numbers written on their backs. The three forms of “lie” are “lie, lay, lain”. The word “thorough” means “complete”. The verb “give back” means “return”. The words “may” and “might” express the idea of “perhaps”. We can see the film any evening next week.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What sort of things do we put in sandwiches?  a) sand, wire, coal  b) ham, cheese, egg  c) paint, soap, wool → **b** (L64)
2. What’s a rain shower?  a) a very cold wind  b) a long period of snow  c) a short period of rain → **c** (L67)
3. What does a mirror do?  a) it reflects light  b) it makes noise  c) it cuts paper → **a** (L67)
4. What happens to grass if there’s no rain?  a) it grows  b) it becomes yellow and dies  c) it becomes blue → **b** (L70)
5. How many centimetres make a metre?  a) ten  b) fifty  c) a hundred → **c** (L73)
6. What do mice like eating?  a) cheese  b) stones  c) wire → **a** (L74)
7. How many inches make a foot?  a) six  b) twelve  c) twenty → **b** (L76)
8. What’s a wall normally built of?  a) sand  b) paper  c) bricks → **c** (L76)
9. Which two colours can be mixed to make purple?  a) yellow and blue  b) red and green  c) red and blue → **c** (L79)
10. Which animal do we get wool from?  a) a cow  b) a sheep  c) a dog → **b** (L82)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. When we ____ the street, I want you both to hold my hand, OK? → **cross** (L69)
2. Some parts of the river are pretty wide, but here it’s very ____ . You can swim across quite easily. → **narrow** (L70)
3. The TV isn’t ____ enough for me. Could you turn it up, please? → **loud** (L71)
4. Brian’s a good boss. He normally points ____ what you’re doing right – not what you’re getting wrong. → **out** (L72)
5. That door won’t open; it’s the exit. We need to find the ____ , which I think is on the other side of the building. → **entrance** (L74)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. We can use the expression “If I were you” to ____ advice.  a) make  b) give  c) suggest → **b** (L64)
2. Jonny, you need to drink less beer, do some exercise, and ____ better care of your health generally.  a) take  b) put  c) make → **a** (L65)
3. Bill and I know each other really well. We met ____ we were little kids.  a) since  b) for  c) when → **c** (L66)
4. You should avoid ____ about what happened if you can. You’re not going to lose your job.  a) worrying  b) that you worry  c) to worry → **a** (L67)
5. You will never persuade ____ you any money.  a) that Tom lends  b) Tom to lend  c) Tom lending → **b** (L67)
6. What’s your reason ____ selling the house? I thought you loved living there.  a) why  b) for  c) you’re → **b** (L68)
7. Just yesterday, fourteen babies ____ in this hospital. It’s a real pleasure to work here.  a) have born  b) born  c) were born → **c** (L68)
8. We closed all the windows ____ the cold air from outside the building couldn’t get in.  a) so that  b) in order to stop  c) to prevent → **c** (L71)
9. I have no influence ____ Rory, I’m afraid. He just does what he wants.  a) for  b) over  c) onto → **b** (L74)
10. Be very careful. This detective isn’t ____ the habit of making mistakes.  a) in  b) under  c) on → **a** (L78)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. In the darkness, we could hear all the mouse moving around, but we couldn’t see them. → **In the darkness, we could hear all the mice moving around, but we couldn’t see them.** (L74)
2. I always have to borrow Fiona a pen during the lessons because she never remembers to bring one with her. → **I always have to lend Fiona a pen during the lessons because she never remembers to bring one with her.** (L75)
3. I can’t find the gold chain that David gave me nowhere. Have you seen it? → **I can’t find the gold chain that David gave me anywhere. Have you seen it?** (L77)
4. Excuse me, sir. I’m afraid no visiters are allowed in this part of the building. You’re going to have to leave. → **Excuse me, sir. I’m afraid no visitors are allowed in this part of the building. You’re going to have to leave.** (L80)
5. This shopping centre is composed with two separate buildings, one on either side of the road, and they’re connected by a bridge. → **This shopping centre is composed of two separate buildings, one on either side of the road, and they’re connected by a bridge.** (L81)
6. What would you buy if I would give you £10,000? → **What would you buy if I gave you £10,000?** (L64, adaptado)
7. My sister and brother aren’t close; they have very little for common. → **My sister and brother aren’t close; they have very little in common.** (L65, adaptado)
8. I’m sorry to make you waiting. I’ll be with you in a minute. → **I’m sorry to keep you waiting. I’ll be with you in a minute.** (L67, adaptado)
9. He said his laptop was faulty but, to my opinion, there’s nothing wrong with it. → **He said his laptop was faulty but, in my opinion, there’s nothing wrong with it.** (L69, adaptado)
10. I think Michela lives with pasta. I never see her eat anything else! → **I think Michela lives on pasta. I never see her eat anything else!** (L70, adaptado)
