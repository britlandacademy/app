# Simulado A · Stage 11 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

Hardly had we arrived when Uncle Bob decided to start singing one of his many songs. I’d never heard of that band prior to visiting the UK. The story is set in Australia but almost all the filming actually took place in California. I have no objection to your friends coming round here as long as you clear up after they’ve gone. We should brush up on our Spanish a bit before we go to Argentina. It’s actually a six-bedroom house, but almost none of the building is visible from the road.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What do we push with our feet to make a bicycle move?  a) the brakes  b) the pedals  c) the saddle → **b** (L175, adaptado)
2. What’s the opposite of the verb “plug in”?  a) unplug  b) replug  c) unlock → **a** (L176)
3. What do we call the jobs we do around the house, like washing the dishes?  a) hobbies  b) sports  c) chores → **c** (L177, adaptado)
4. What do we call closing one eye quickly as a signal?  a) blinking  b) winking  c) staring → **b** (L178)
5. What do we call the mark that stays on the skin after a deep cut has healed?  a) a scar  b) a stain  c) a tattoo → **a** (L179, adaptado)
6. What do we use to catch fish?  a) a ladder  b) a spade  c) a fishing rod → **c** (L180, adaptado)
7. What’s another word for a sitting room?  a) a garage  b) a lounge  c) a basement → **b** (L181)
8. What do police cars use to warn people with a loud noise?  a) sirens  b) bells  c) whistles → **a** (L182, adaptado)
9. What are the three forms of “weep”?  a) weep, weeped, weeped  b) weep, wept, wept  c) weep, weep, wept → **b** (L181)
10. What do we remove from a shirt with an iron?  a) creases  b) colours  c) buttons → **a** (L190, adaptado)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. I know Darren ate my chicken sandwich yesterday, but I ____ he tries to deny it. → **bet** (L176)
2. Jane’s enjoying herself in Germany immensely, but she’s by no ____ planning to settle there. → **means** (L176)
3. Ask Janet to repeat things if necessary. She has a ____ to speak really quickly when she’s excited. → **tendency** (L178)
4. You can’t hand in your ____ now, Sam. The company’s never needed your skills more! → **notice** (L180)
5. On ____ of the captain and all the crew, I’d like to thank you for flying with Icarus Airlines this morning. → **behalf** (L184)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. Tom’s so creative. How does he ____ up with all his musical ideas?  a) bring  b) come  c) keep → **b** (L175)
2. Harry was making no friends at college, but it never occurred ____ that it might be his fault.  a) to him  b) his mind  c) at all → **a** (L177)
3. A guitar ____ of two main parts: the body and the neck.  a) contains  b) comprises  c) consists → **c** (L177)
4. All staff members are entitled ____ a 25% discount on company products.  a) for  b) to  c) with → **b** (L178)
5. Dan broke his arm climbing at the weekend. Needless ____ , he won’t be playing football with us this evening.  a) to say  b) we say  c) saying → **a** (L179)
6. Put these biscuits on the table now, and I’ll keep the rest ____ reserve for when Dad gets home later.  a) as  b) in  c) for → **b** (L180)
7. Please dispose ____ all your rubbish carefully, using the bins provided.  a) of  b) away  c) for → **a** (L181)
8. I’d have been late if you hadn’t ____ me a lift this morning.  a) taken  b) caught  c) given → **c** (L183)
9. Nothing on this lunch menu really appeals ____ me. Could we try somewhere else?  a) for  b) with  c) to → **c** (L185)
10. All the documents seem to be ____ order, so, as long as you’re happy, you can put your signature on the bottom line there.  a) in  b) to  c) at → **a** (L187)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. I can’t claim to have any strong opinions on this issue, so I’m quite content to go down with the view of the majority. → **I can’t claim to have any strong opinions on this issue, so I’m quite content to go along with the view of the majority.** (L175)
2. How can they hope to govern effectively if they don’t discuss urgent matters in a daily basis? → **How can they hope to govern effectively if they don’t discuss urgent matters on a daily basis?** (L175, adaptado)
3. Jan was sentenced for two years in prison for something that isn’t even a crime in our country! → **Jan was sentenced to two years in prison for something that isn’t even a crime in our country!** (L176, adaptado)
4. Justin realized that, by bearing out the order his commanding officer had just given him, he’d be committing murder. → **Justin realized that, by carrying out the order his commanding officer had just given him, he’d be committing murder.** (L178, adaptado)
5. How can you be surprised? Of course you had the sack! You poured coffee over your boss! → **How can you be surprised? Of course you got the sack! You poured coffee over your boss!** (L178, adaptado)
6. When Luca saw the police come in, he made straight on the exit, but they managed to catch him. → **When Luca saw the police come in, he made straight for the exit, but they managed to catch him.** (L182, adaptado)
7. Coming back home from the party on your own is completely out of the matter, Tom. I’ll come to fetch you. → **Coming back home from the party on your own is completely out of the question, Tom. I’ll come to fetch you.** (L184, adaptado)
8. Kim is a very sensible person except when it goes to relationships! → **Kim is a very sensible person except when it comes to relationships!** (L185, adaptado)
9. I can’t believe you trusted Charles when he joined the company. I saw by him immediately. → **I can’t believe you trusted Charles when he joined the company. I saw through him immediately.** (L186, adaptado)
10. The company is closing ten of its stores in Europe, but no staff will be sent redundant. → **The company is closing ten of its stores in Europe, but no staff will be made redundant.** (L190, adaptado)
