## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. ____ you lost your job, what would you do?  (**Supposing** / Suppose / Supposed)
2. It gets ____ as we go towards the north of Brazil.  (more hotter / more hot / **hotter and hotter**)
3. It takes me about 30 minutes to get ____ to go out in the morning.  (already / **ready** / all ready)
4. If I say that Michael Jackson was tall, thin, had black hair; I´m ____ him.  (describe / **describing** / description)
5. She went home ____ the lesson was over.  (soon as / as soon / **as soon as**)
6. ____ the door, please!  (**Shut** / Shut up / Closed)
7. I´m very good at ____ people after not having seen them for years.  (**recognizing** / recognize / recognized)
8. I´d say I spoke English ____ well now.  (would rather / **rather** / rare)
9. It´s healthier to take the ____ to get to a room that is on the 1st or 2nd floor.  (upstairs / downstairs / **stairs**)
10. I wouldn´t like to be a ____.  (**sailor** / sail / sailing)
11. It´s ____ to judge people by their appearance.  (fair / fairly / **unfair**)
12. It´s a great ____ for most people to travel to other countries.  (**pleasure** / pleasant / please)
13. The time now is 6pm. If I went out of the room and said I´ll be back ____ an hour I´d return at any time between 6pm and 7pm.´  (in / **within** / into)
14. You have never ____ in public.  (sing / sang / **sung**)
15. I sold my house for half its price. My ____ was $10.000.  (profit / lost / **loss**)
16. Last night I ____ my bicycle around town.  (road / **rode** / ride)
17. The elbow is one of the ____ of the arm.  (**joints** / join / joins)
18. ____ is another word for midday.  (Moon / Mud / **Noon**)
19. He signed a formal ____ yesterday.  (state / station / **statement**)
20. ____ people often get into fights.  (Drank / **Drunk** / Drunked)
