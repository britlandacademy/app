# Simulado B · Stage 12 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

Don’t worry about Eddie. I’m sure he has everything under control. I must try harder not to take offence whenever people criticize my work. Aunt Mary drank far too much at the barbecue, and was stumbling around the garden on her own. She was lucky not to fall over and hurt herself. We have several issues to deal with in this meeting, but first and foremost is the matter of staff pay. Mary didn’t exactly take to me at first, but she doesn’t seem to mind me now. Nobody was in a hurry to get back to work, so we lingered over lunch a little bit longer than we should have done.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What do we call the person who controls a tennis match?  a) a coach  b) an umpire  c) a goalkeeper → **b** (L194, adaptado)
2. What do we call information that must be kept secret?  a) confident  b) confused  c) confidential → **c** (L195, adaptado)
3. What do we call keeping your teeth clean to avoid fillings?  a) oral hygiene  b) oral surgery  c) oral history → **a** (L196, adaptado)
4. What do we call the part of the news that tells us tomorrow’s weather?  a) the forehead  b) the forecast  c) the foreword → **b** (L196, adaptado)
5. What do you do when you walk unsteadily and almost fall?  a) you stumble  b) you stir  c) you stamp → **a** (L200, adaptado)
6. What do we call a situation of complete disorder?  a) calm  b) chaos  c) charm → **b** (L200, adaptado)
7. What does a person bear when they stay angry at someone for a long time because of something in the past?  a) a grudge  b) a grind  c) a grunt → **a** (L204, adaptado)
8. What do you do with your shoulders to show you don’t know or don’t care?  a) you shake them  b) you shrug them  c) you shut them → **b** (L202, adaptado)
9. What do we call a flat with one main room that is the bedroom and living room together?  a) a studio flat  b) a terraced house  c) a bungalow → **a** (L198, adaptado)
10. What does it mean to enrol on a course?  a) to cancel it  b) to finish it  c) to register to take it → **c** (L195, adaptado)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. I’d better jot ____ your name and address in case I forget. → **down** (L195)
2. I can see three men standing in the car park but I can’t make ____ who they are. → **out** (L199)
3. My husband said we’d won the lottery but, at first, I couldn’t ____ the information in. He had to tell me three times! → **take** (L199)
4. The two men ____ to eight years each by the judge yesterday will probably be free to leave prison after about five years. → **sentenced** (L203)
5. I’m afraid I can’t personally answer your question. Let me put you ____ to our technical department. → **through** (L196)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. Regardless ____ all the objections, the president refused to change his mind.  a) of  b) to  c) than → **a** (L192)
2. Could you possibly stop ____ at the supermarket on your way home and get a few things?  a) off  b) over  c) round → **a** (L197)
3. I wouldn’t ____ Ken’s remarks about your dress sense too seriously if I were you; I’m sure he didn’t mean it.  a) feel  b) take  c) make → **b** (L197)
4. We’d better use this milk today; it’s going to start ____ off pretty soon.  a) giving  b) turning  c) going → **c** (L198)
5. Your mother thinks your behaviour was out of order, and I ____ to agree.  a) incline  b) am inclined  c) would incline → **b** (L199)
6. We are highly trained, but our aim is always to manage a situation without needing to resort ____ physical combat.  a) with  b) to  c) by → **b** (L201)
7. Harry is so ____ ; he never remembers anything I ask him to do!  a) vague-minded  b) absent-minded  c) vacant-minded → **b** (L202)
8. Michael’s very good with colours. He should ____ his hand at painting.  a) test  b) turn  c) try → **c** (L203)
9. If you make the right decisions now, you stand ____ a significant amount of money.  a) to make  b) making  c) up to making → **a** (L206)
10. Nobody played badly in the match but, on the other hand, nobody stood ____ for special praise either.  a) above  b) off  c) out → **c** (L206)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. In event of fire, staff members should leave the building by the nearest exit. → **In case of fire, staff members should leave the building by the nearest exit.** (L192, adaptado)
2. Our flight’s leaving from Boarding 26, but we’ve got lots of time to get there. → **Our flight’s leaving from Gate 26, but we’ve got lots of time to get there.** (L193, adaptado)
3. Why did the soldiers set fire at the train station? → **Why did the soldiers set fire to the train station?** (L199, adaptado)
4. There were countless times I felt like giving up, but I didn’t. I drove at it, year after year, until I finally fulfilled my dream. → **There were countless times I felt like giving up, but I didn’t. I kept at it, year after year, until I finally fulfilled my dream.** (L201, adaptado)
5. On one, the children went up on stage to receive their prizes. → **One by one, the children went up on stage to receive their prizes.** (L201, adaptado)
6. Take the weekend to think things up and let me know your decision on Monday. → **Take the weekend to think things over and let me know your decision on Monday.** (L202, adaptado)
7. I can’t believe you dozed out during the speeches at your own wedding reception! → **I can’t believe you dozed off during the speeches at your own wedding reception!** (L202, adaptado)
8. I was sure my application would be sent down, but I’ve just been invited for a second interview. → **I was sure my application would be turned down, but I’ve just been invited for a second interview.** (L203, adaptado)
9. I know Harry’s plan to open his own restaurant will go off; he always succeeds in the end. → **I know Harry’s plan to open his own restaurant will come off; he always succeeds in the end.** (L204, adaptado)
10. The holiday was good overall, but the hotel we stayed in failed to stand up to my expectations. → **The holiday was good overall, but the hotel we stayed in failed to live up to my expectations.** (L206, adaptado)
