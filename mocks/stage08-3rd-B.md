# Simulado B · Stage 8 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

A stream is a small river. By unsettled weather, we mean weather that’s constantly changing. Spiders don’t make honey; bees do! The referee blew his whistle to stop the game. The plural of “deer” is “deer”. We call the word “the” the definite article. Humans have four limbs. We use an axe to split wood. Let me know if you come across my sunglasses anywhere. Don’t look at my phone. Mind your own business! You needn’t have cut all the vegetables. Carla’s got a rather odd sense of humour, hasn’t she?

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What’s a housemate or flatmate?  a) someone who we live with  b) someone who we work for  c) someone who we marry → **a** (L124)
2. What artificial parts of the body can science provide us with?  a) wings, tails and horns  b) arms, legs, teeth and hair  c) hearts made of glass → **b** (L126)
3. What are the articles in English?  a) “the”, “a” and “an”  b) “is”, “am” and “are”  c) “this”, “that” and “those” → **a** (L130)
4. What does the word “afterwards” express?  a) the idea of “before that”  b) the idea of “after that”  c) the idea of “never” → **b** (L132)
5. What are the three forms of “bind”?  a) bind, binded, binded  b) bind, band, bound  c) bind, bound, bound → **c** (L134)
6. What does the verb “swear” mean?  a) to swim very fast  b) to sleep a lot  c) to say very bad words → **c** (L137)
7. What are the three forms of “swear”?  a) swear, swore, sworn  b) swear, sweared, sweared  c) swear, swore, swore → **a** (L137)
8. What do we mean by public services?  a) shops, banks and cinemas  b) education, healthcare, public transport and rubbish collection  c) cars, houses and boats → **b** (L137)
9. What do we call a reduction in price?  a) a discount  b) a trend  c) a loan → **a** (L121, adaptado)
10. What do we call it when workers stop working because they want better pay?  a) a holiday  b) a strike  c) a prize → **b** (L123, adaptado)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. ____ to the hotel manager, the local bars can get crowded after 9 p.m. → **According** (L122)
2. I’ve got my driving test tomorrow, so keep your fingers ____ for me! → **crossed** (L125)
3. Joana has a huge ____ over anyone else who applies for the job in Brazil because she already speaks the language. → **advantage** (L128)
4. We’ve got half an hour to wait till the coach comes. What shall we do to ____ the time? → **pass** (L133)
5. Are you sure you can ____ to buy that handbag, Adele? It’s very pretty, but it’s £7,000! → **afford** (L134)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. There’s no point ____ driving to the beach if it’s going to be cold and cloudy.  a) in  b) for  c) with → **a** (L121)
2. Are you planning to ____ part in the summer concert this term, Ms Hodgson?  a) play  b) join  c) take → **c** (L122)
3. I didn’t tell Billy ____ when he dropped the plates; he was only trying to help me with the washing up.  a) down  b) off  c) away → **b** (L126)
4. If you hadn’t insisted ____ leaving early, I’m certain we would have missed that flight!  a) by  b) for  c) on → **c** (L126)
5. I moved to the country for work, but war ____ just two months later.  a) declared  b) broke out  c) was opened → **b** (L127)
6. Buying a new car would be expensive, but we need to ____ into account that it’d last us for many years.  a) take  b) keep  c) add → **a** (L131)
7. Alessia ____ up on a farm in southern Italy with her brothers.  a) brought  b) grew  c) set → **b** (L133)
8. There’s a definite ____ among young people towards finding employment after leaving school instead of going to university.  a) opinion  b) attitude  c) trend → **c** (L135)
9. Chickens can fly but, so ____ as I know, not far.  a) good  b) much  c) far → **c** (L136)
10. Little ____ little, our new house is feeling more like a family home.  a) then  b) by  c) on → **b** (L138)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. The cowboys haven’t found all the cattle, but they’re making a lot of progresses and have caught more than half of them already. → **The cowboys haven’t found all the cattle, but they’re making a lot of progress and have caught more than half of them already.** (L128)
2. It normally takes me twenty-five minutes to clean the car, but now, with my injured arm, I have to spend over a hour doing it, and I can’t even do it well. → **It normally takes me twenty-five minutes to clean the car, but now, with my injured arm, I have to spend over an hour doing it, and I can’t even do it well.** (L130)
3. Come down, Mary. You know that’s not true. → **Come on, Mary. You know that’s not true.** (L121, adaptado)
4. Write the address carefully and don’t let out the postcode. → **Write the address carefully and don’t leave out the postcode.** (L124, adaptado)
5. We quarrelled what had gone wrong for a very long time. → **We discussed what had gone wrong for a very long time.** (L125, adaptado)
6. I love my mother-by-law. She’s a wonderful human being. → **I love my mother-in-law. She’s a wonderful human being.** (L127, adaptado)
7. With regards travel and accommodation, the company will pay for everything. → **As regards travel and accommodation, the company will pay for everything.** (L128, adaptado)
8. I’m driving there, so just stay where you are and I’ll come and lift you on the way. → **I’m driving there, so just stay where you are and I’ll come and pick you on the way.** (L129, adaptado)
9. My work as a police officer involves me to go into dangerous situations that people would normally advise you to avoid. → **My work as a police officer involves going into dangerous situations that people would normally advise you to avoid.** (L132, adaptado)
10. I wish I had put up boxing years ago. It’s a great way to stay fit. → **I wish I had taken up boxing years ago. It’s a great way to stay fit.** (L136, adaptado)
