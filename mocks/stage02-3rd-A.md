# Simulado A · Stage 2 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), Yes/No (15), múltipla escolha (12), correção de erros (8) = 50 pontos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item (“novo” = item novo, no máximo 10%).

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

Both of us are speaking English. I prefer tea to coffee. Seven is half of fourteen. My eyes are the same colour as your eyes. Mr Brown is married; he has got a wife. There is a light hanging from the ceiling. Paris is in the north of France. We can’t walk without moving our legs. My watch is made of steel. I don’t want anything to drink at the moment. I eat three meals a day. Windows are made of glass. The time is half past nine. A town is larger than a village. Please take your hands out of your pockets.

(Frase nova no ditado: “Windows are made of glass.”)

## Part 3 · Multiple choice (12)

1. I prefer tea ____ coffee.  a) to  b) at  c) for → **a** (L13)
2. Your eyes are the same colour ____ my eyes.  a) of  b) as  c) from → **b** (L14)
3. Mr Brown is ____ .  a) husband  b) father  c) married → **c** (L15)
4. Tell me ____ places you like in London.  a) some the  b) some of the  c) some of → **b** (L16)
5. We smell with our ____ .  a) ears  b) eyes  c) noses → **c** (L17)
6. I’m not good ____ learning languages.  a) at  b) of  c) in → **a** (L19)
7. Julia is ____ new shoes today. I like them!  a) wearing  b) carrying  c) using → **a** (L20)
8. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. I’m counting ____ five.  a) from one to  b) first one last  c) between one and → **a** (L21)
9. I eat three meals ____ day.  a) for  b) a  c) in → **b** (L23)
10. The table is next ____ the window.  a) by  b) at  c) to → **c** (L24)
11. We cannot ____ phone calls during the lesson.  a) –  b) make  c) do → **b** (L25)
12. A town is ____ than a village.  a) largest  b) large  c) larger → **c** (L27)

## Part 4 · Error correction (8)

1. Both us are speaking English. → **Both of us are speaking English.** (L13)
2. My eyes are the same colour of your eyes. → **My eyes are the same colour as your eyes.** (L14)
3. Do you go home in car after your English lessons? → **Do you go home by car after your English lessons?** (L15)
4. Tell me some the places you like in London. → **Tell me some of the places you like in London.** (L16)
5. There is few people in a small village. → **There are few people in a small village.** (L17)
6. Why have you got more rice that me? → **Why have you got more rice than me?** (L19)
7. I don’t want anything to drink in the moment. → **I don’t want anything to drink at the moment.** (L22)
8. She wears glasses because she can’t see good. → **She wears glasses because she can’t see well.** (L23)
