## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. I generally ____ Portuguese.  (speaking / **speak**)
2. Do you ____ that book?  (**read** / reading)
3. He ____ in English.  (**writes** / write)
4. I can't speak ____ opening my mouth.  (with / **without**)
5. Does she ____ home after the lesson?  (goes / **go**)
6. I don't sit completely ____ in the lesson.  (**still** / steel)
7. We ____ with our eyes.  (write / **read**)
8. ____ me the name of the capital of England.  (Can / **Tell**)
9. Both of ____ are sitting.  (**us** / we)
10. The words “thank you” mean ____ in Portuguese.  (**obrigado** / até logo)
11. He ____ got two heads, he's only got one head.  (don't / **hasn't**)
12. The French are ____ the Russians.  (the same as / **different from**)
13. Most people write with their ____ hand.  (**right** / left)
14. It isn't ____ hat.  (**Mr. Brown's** / Mr. Brown is)
15. I ____ glasses.  (use / **wear**)
16. I ____ before the lesson.  (**sit down** / stand up)
17. I come to school ____ car.  (with / **by**)
18. It's generally ____ for people to write with their left hand.  (easy / **difficult**)
19. Mr. Brown's got a ____.  (husband / **wife**)
20. Her father's wife's name is ____.  (**Mary** / John)
