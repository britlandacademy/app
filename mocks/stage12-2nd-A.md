## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (30)

1. If this company hopes to grow, there'll need to be an ____ in the range of products.  (expand / expanded / **expansion**)
2. This magazine is currently the only one ____ that makes any profit for the company.  (publishing / **publication** / publish)
3. The phone ____ is now published online to save printing costs.  (**directory** / directed / directing)
4. Peter's quiet in the classroom but he's a lot more ____ outside.  (**talkative** / talk / talking)
5. The biggest ____, with over a thousand inhabitants, was next to the river.  (settle / **settlement** / settled)
6. Life today is not ____ to that of a thousand years ago; it's much easier now.  (compare / comparison / **comparable**)
7. The sales assistants in that store can be very ____, so be careful!  (persuade / **persuasive** / persuaded)
8. I've had a rather ____ day so far. Let's hope it gets better!  (problem / **problematic** / problematization)
9. I'm not sure whether the price he's quoted me for the job is ____ of tax or not.  (**inclusive** / include / included)
10. Anne's ____ features are so striking she could be a model if she wanted.  (**facial** / face / facing)
11. I don't personally think he has the ____ to succeed in such a demanding job.  (capableness / capable / **capability**)
12. I offered him some money for my train ticket but he was very ____ that he should pay for it.  (insisting / insist / **insistent**)
13. He was really very ill last week but he's become ____ better in the last few days.  (note / **noticeably** / noticeable)
14. The shop she's buying her wedding dress from is so ____ that you need an appointment.  (exclude / **exclusive** / excluding)
15. Thanks for being so ____ of what I was doing; it made me feel a lot more confident.  (**supportive** / supported / support)
16. This insect can be extremely ____ if it gets into your garden.  (destroy / **destructive** / destructing)
17. Your ____ in this matter would be greatly appreciated.  (cooperate / cooperated / **cooperation**)
18. What a ____ day! I hope tomorrow's better!  (disaster / disastering / **disastrous**)
19. The soldiers spent many hours preparing for their annual ____ by the king.  (inspect / inspected / **inspection**)
20. I tend to give money to ____ more often that I used to.  (beg / **beggars** / begging)
21. The phrasal verb cut back means to ____.  (**reduce something** / leave something out of / manage to do a particular thing)
22. The phrasal verb give up means to ____.  (**stop trying to do something** / try hard to get something / insist on something)
23. The phrasal verb join in means to ____.  (invent an explanation / **become involved** / stop using a telephone)
24. The phrasal verb hold up means to ____.  (start doing something / **make someone wait** / read something quickly)
25. The phrasal verb break o means to ____.  (**end a relationship** / write a mark on a document / decide not to do something)
26. The phrasal verb take over means to ____.  (losing consciousness / **assume control** / avoid doing something)
27. The phrasal verb jot down means to ____.  (trick someone / be unkind to someone / **write something**)
28. The phrasal verb get through means to ____.  (put something in safe place / be fired / **deal with a difficult situation**)
29. The phrasal verb stop over means to ____.  (think about someone / **visit a place** / arrive in a place)
30. The phrasal verb live on means to ____.  (be important / make a sound / **eat a particular kind of food**)
