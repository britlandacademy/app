# Simulado B · Stage 2 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), Yes/No (15), múltipla escolha (12), correção de erros (8) = 50 pontos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item (“novo” = item novo, no máximo 10%).

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

I’m telling you my name. Half of the people in this town are men. We stand up after the lesson. The French are different from the Russians. Mrs Brown’s husband is standing in front of the house. I’m learning English from you. There are few people in a small village. There are twelve months in a year. We wear a watch on our wrist. The red book is small and the blue one is large. Today is Thursday; the day after tomorrow will be Saturday. The small boy goes to bed before his sister. I want to live for a hundred years. Rob likes playing the piano.

## Part 3 · Multiple choice (12)

1. Both ____ us are speaking English.  a) of  b) to  c) at → **a** (L13)
2. The Spanish word “pantalones” ____ “trousers” in English.  a) mean  b) meaning  c) means → **c** (L13)
3. Mr Brown’s ____ .  a) got shoes black  b) shoes are black  c) wear black shoes → **b** (L14)
4. We can’t walk ____ moving our legs.  a) without  b) with  c) during → **a** (L16)
5. Rob is sitting ____ your left-hand side.  a) on  b) at  c) by → **a** (L17)
6. We use “into” for a thing that moves from one place to ____ .  a) other  b) the other  c) another → **c** (L18)
7. The time is half ____ nine.  a) to  b) past  c) by → **b** (L20)
8. This is your pen; it’s yours. That is my pen; it’s ____ .  a) my  b) mine  c) me → **b** (L21)
9. How ____ are your English lessons? Do they last fifty minutes?  a) much  b) during  c) long → **c** (L22)
10. Josef is putting the food into the ____ .  a) plate  b) bowl  c) table → **b** (L23)
11. I have ____ friends, but I have ____ friends than Harry!  a) most / less  b) much / fewer  c) few / more → **c** (L24)
12. Rob likes playing the ____ .  a) toys  b) piano  c) tennis → **b** (L27)

## Part 4 · Error correction (8)

1. I prefer tea than coffee. → **I prefer tea to coffee.** (L13)
2. The Spanish word “pantalones” mean “trousers” in English. → **The Spanish word “pantalones” means “trousers” in English.** (L13)
3. I’m not teaching you English; I’m learn English from you. → **I’m not teaching you English; I’m learning English from you.** (L14)
4. My eyes are different colours. One eye is blue, and the another eye is grey. → **My eyes are different colours. One eye is blue, and the other eye is grey.** (L15)
5. Tomas: “Who jacket is this?” → **Tomas: “Whose jacket is this?”** (L24)
6. I’m not enough tall to touch the ceiling. → **I’m not tall enough to touch the ceiling.** (L25)
7. I have less friends than Harry. → **I have fewer friends than Harry.** (L24)
8. The small boy generally goes in bed before his sister. → **The small boy generally goes to bed before his sister.** (L27)
