# Simulado A · Stage 5 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

We put ham, cheese and egg in sandwiches. The three forms of “run” are “run, ran, run”. The opposite of “keep a promise” is “break a promise”. A rain shower is a short period of rain. A mirror reflects light. If there’s no rain, grass becomes yellow and then it dies. The past of “throw” is “threw”. A hundred centimetres make a metre. Mice like eating cheese. We find sand on a beach or in a desert. A wall is normally built of bricks. Twelve inches make a foot. Red and blue can be mixed to make purple. We get wool from a sheep.

2. What does the verb “give back” mean?  a) borrow  b) return  c) lose → **b** (L73)
3. What do we use wire for?  a) to cut paper  b) to drink water  c) to carry electricity → **c** (L76)
4. What does the word “towards” mean?  a) in the direction of  b) after  c) under → **a** (L80)
5. How many letters is the English alphabet composed of?  a) twenty-four  b) twenty-six  c) twenty-eight → **b** (L81)
6. What’s the past of “fly”?  a) flied  b) flown  c) flew → **c** (L82)
7. What are the three forms of “lie”?  a) lie, lied, lied  b) lie, lay, lain  c) lie, lay, lay → **b** (L72)
8. What do footballers’ shirts have written on their backs?  a) pictures  b) letters  c) names and numbers → **c** (L82)
9. What do we sometimes see and hear in a storm?  a) snow and ice  b) lightning and thunder  c) sun and wind → **b** (L77)
10. What does the word “mad” mean?  a) happy  b) tired  c) angry → **c** (L74)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. What would you buy if I ____ you £10,000?  a) gave  b) give  c) would give → **a** (L64)
2. My sister and brother aren’t close; they have very little ____ common.  a) for  b) of  c) in → **c** (L65)
3. You don’t need to drive this fast, Anna. We’ve got ____ time to get to the airport.  a) enough  b) plenty  c) too much → **b** (L66)
4. I’m sorry to ____ you waiting. I’ll be with you in a minute.  a) make  b) ask  c) keep → **c** (L67)
5. Many soldiers ____ killed during the war, and nobody can do anything to stop it.  a) were  b) have been  c) are → **a** (L68)
6. He said his laptop was faulty but, ____ my opinion, there’s nothing wrong with it.  a) after  b) to  c) in → **c** (L69)
7. I think Michela lives ____ pasta. I never see her eat anything else!  a) with  b) on  c) by → **b** (L70)
8. Let me ____ that bag for you. I’m very strong!  a) carry  b) carrying  c) to carry → **a** (L77)
9. Would you mind ____ the shopping tomorrow instead of today?  a) doing  b) I do  c) to do → **a** (L80)
10. ____ she had plenty of opportunities, Deborah never visited any foreign countries.  a) In spite of  b) Although  c) Despite → **b** (L75)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. Both these paints are white, but please don’t mixture them together. I bought this one especially for painting the bathroom. → **Both these paints are white, but please don’t mix them together. I bought this one especially for painting the bathroom.** (L65)
2. I don’t know whether I should go to the police. What would you do if you were at my situation? → **I don’t know whether I should go to the police. What would you do if you were in my situation?** (L67)
3. All the snacks we eat in my family are made for my mother. They aren’t very tasty, but we never tell her; she loves making them. → **All the snacks we eat in my family are made by my mother. They aren’t very tasty, but we never tell her; she loves making them.** (L68)
4. Sam and I enjoyed us very much at the zoo, Aunt Margaret. Thank you for taking us. → **Sam and I enjoyed ourselves very much at the zoo, Aunt Margaret. Thank you for taking us.** (L70)
5. I didn’t think you ever wore that old pullover, so I throwed it away when I was cleaning the house last weekend. → **I didn’t think you ever wore that old pullover, so I threw it away when I was cleaning the house last weekend.** (L70)
6. We can use the expression “If I were you” to make advice. → **We can use the expression “If I were you” to give advice.** (L64, adaptado)
7. Jonny, you need to drink less beer, do some exercise, and put better care of your health generally. → **Jonny, you need to drink less beer, do some exercise, and take better care of your health generally.** (L65, adaptado)
8. You should avoid to worry about what happened if you can. You’re not going to lose your job. → **You should avoid worrying about what happened if you can. You’re not going to lose your job.** (L67, adaptado)
9. Just yesterday, fourteen babies born in this hospital. It’s a real pleasure to work here. → **Just yesterday, fourteen babies were born in this hospital. It’s a real pleasure to work here.** (L68, adaptado)
10. I have no influence for Rory, I’m afraid. He just does what he wants. → **I have no influence over Rory, I’m afraid. He just does what he wants.** (L74, adaptado)
