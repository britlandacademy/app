# Simulado A · Stage 6 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

The three forms of “choose” are “choose, chose, chosen”. The past of “ride” is “rode”. The past of “fall” is “fell”. The three forms of “blow” are “blow, blew, blown”. Books are made of paper. “Ought” is the only exception to this rule. We call the words “who”, “which” and “that” relative pronouns. The expression “every now and again” means “sometimes but not often”. Dolphins are mammals, not fish. I’m losing weight by going to the gym. Could you lend me a hand getting this piano up the stairs, please? Geoff’s dinner consisted of baked beans and a slice of toast. We can meet any time next week. Let me know which day you’d prefer.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What’d happen if someone hit a policeman?  a) they’d be arrested  b) they’d be paid  c) they’d be married → **a** (L85)
2. What’s another word for midday?  a) night  b) noon  c) morning → **b** (L86)
3. What are two other words for “ill”?  a) happy and glad  b) tired and bored  c) sick and unwell → **c** (L88)
4. What can we use rope for?  a) climbing mountains  b) drinking water  c) writing letters → **a** (L90)
5. What are the opposites of “overcook” and “overpay”?  a) recook and repay  b) undercook and underpay  c) uncook and unpay → **b** (L92)
6. What’s mud?  a) a mixture of earth and water  b) a kind of fruit  c) a piece of land → **a** (L94)
7. What’s another name for a chemist’s?  a) a bakery  b) a library  c) a pharmacy → **c** (L95)
8. How many grams make a kilogram?  a) a hundred  b) a thousand  c) a million → **b** (L96)
9. What’s an island?  a) a piece of land completely surrounded by water  b) a very big city  c) a kind of boat → **a** (L97)
10. How many days does a fortnight consist of?  a) seven  b) ten  c) fourteen → **c** (L101)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. I’ve just been told the hotel lift is broken, so we’ll have to carry our luggage up the ____ . → **stairs** (L84)
2. Electric ____ are very uncommon in this country. Everyone just heats water for tea on the hob. → **kettles** (L86)
3. Almost everything you heard Charles say during the meeting was a ____ . That man never tells the truth. → **lie** (L90)
4. Nobody woke me up this morning and I ____ ; I didn’t wake up till almost ten o’clock and arrived for work two hours late! → **overslept** (L92)
5. Look at that cat. It’s sleeping in a really strange ____ with its front legs above its head and its back legs completely straight. → **position** (L93)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. We ____ breakfast in a hurry and immediately ran to the train station.  a) ate  b) had eaten  c) were eating → **a** (L83)
2. I’d like you to start working as ____ as you arrive. Don’t spend the first hour chatting!  a) straight  b) soon  c) directly → **b** (L83)
3. Of course I can roast a chicken. You should have more faith ____ my cooking skills!  a) by  b) for  c) in → **c** (L84)
4. When’s your boyfriend arriving? I can’t wait ____ him!  a) to meet  b) for meeting  c) till I’ll meet → **a** (L85)
5. ____ Paula use to live in Spain before she moved to England? She speaks the language so well.  a) Might  b) Had  c) Did → **c** (L86)
6. The police officer said he ____ me if I didn’t do exactly what he said.  a) would arrest  b) arrested  c) was arresting → **a** (L89)
7. We would have gone climbing if ____ any ropes with us.  a) we took  b) we’d taken  c) we’d have taken → **b** (L91)
8. There is a lift in the building, but the property we’re going to look at is on the ground ____ , so you wouldn’t need to use it.  a) level  b) floor  c) flat → **b** (L92)
9. Agata ____ about ten minutes trying to open the ketchup bottle but couldn’t.  a) had spent  b) spent  c) has been spending → **b** (L94)
10. This summer holiday, you’re happy to visit somewhere you’ve been to before, ____ ?  a) don’t you  b) haven’t you  c) aren’t you → **c** (L95)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. Last year, our company made a small losing, but this year, we’ve made a very healthy profit. → **Last year, our company made a small loss, but this year, we’ve made a very healthy profit.** (L85)
2. Three of our players hurted themselves during the game on Tuesday, but we hope they’ll all be fine for the next match. → **Three of our players hurt themselves during the game on Tuesday, but we hope they’ll all be fine for the next match.** (L88)
3. If I were you, I’d book an appointment to see the doctor about your earach; it’s getting worse, isn’t it? → **If I were you, I’d book an appointment to see the doctor about your earache; it’s getting worse, isn’t it?** (L91)
4. I don’t know why we bought Chloe a bicycle. She’s only rides it once or twice since we got it; it’s just there in the garden getting wet. → **I don’t know why we bought Chloe a bicycle. She’s only ridden it once or twice since we got it; it’s just there in the garden getting wet.** (L93)
5. They had asked us in several previous occasions not to park outside their house, so I wasn’t surprised when they complained to us about it. → **They had asked us on several previous occasions not to park outside their house, so I wasn’t surprised when they complained to us about it.** (L99)
6. As there was no future in agricultural work, Solomon encouraged his sons for moving to the industrial north of the country to find work. → **As there was no future in agricultural work, Solomon encouraged his sons to move to the industrial north of the country to find work.** (L83, adaptado)
7. Which do you consider being the most important: health, money or love? → **Which do you consider to be the most important: health, money or love?** (L89, adaptado)
8. The children played turns to see who could say the English alphabet the fastest! → **The children took turns to see who could say the English alphabet the fastest!** (L96, adaptado)
9. Eduardo has offered me £100 by exchange for helping him to move house. → **Eduardo has offered me £100 in exchange for helping him to move house.** (L100, adaptado)
10. I’m sorry but this plan doesn’t look like sense. We need to go away and think about it more. → **I’m sorry but this plan doesn’t make much sense. We need to go away and think about it more.** (L101, adaptado)
