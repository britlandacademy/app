# Simulado B · Stage 4 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

A bike is either a bicycle or a motorbike. The future of “you go” is “you will go”. We use ice for putting in drinks to cool them. The duration of a football match is ninety minutes. The opposite of “heaven” is “hell”. The past of “have” is “had”. The plural of “wife” is “wives”. The opposite of the verb “to lose” is “to find”. We find a keyhole in a door. A painter holds a paintbrush in his hand. The three forms of “sing” are “sing, sang, sung”. A film star is a famous actor or actress. The only meal I can’t go without is breakfast. We walked along the river for about an hour.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What’s the opposite of “ill”?  a) well  b) tired  c) busy → **a** (L45)
2. What do cars do when traffic lights change from red to green?  a) stop  b) turn off  c) start moving → **c** (L46)
3. What colour’s blood?  a) blue  b) red  c) green → **b** (L47)
4. What’s the past of “can”?  a) could  b) cans  c) canned → **a** (L49)
5. What are the three forms of “come”?  a) come, comed, comed  b) come, came, came  c) come, came, come → **c** (L52)
6. What do we call a bad dream?  a) a holiday  b) a nightmare  c) a drawing → **b** (L54)
7. What do we call the arms of a tree?  a) branches  b) keys  c) legs → **a** (L55)
8. What do Americans call the sport that British people call football?  a) rugby  b) soccer  c) tennis → **b** (L55)
9. What do we call a picture that an artist draws?  a) a painting  b) a painter  c) a drawing → **c** (L57)
10. What are the three forms of “win”?  a) win, wan, wan  b) win, won, won  c) win, win, wan → **b** (L63)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. Hairdressers use ____ to cut hair. → **scissors** (L47)
2. This pullover costs £150, but I don’t think it’s ____ as much as that. → **worth** (L46)
3. Can you help me look ____ my keys, please. I can’t leave home without them. → **for** (L58)
4. Kayla, stop pulling the door. You have to ____ it to open it! → **push** (L55)
5. Please don’t ask me to dance. I have no ____ of rhythm. → **sense** (L59)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. Goodbye! I’m going now. I’ll see you in two ____ time.  a) weeks  b) week’s  c) weeks’ → **c** (L46)
2. I’m hungry enough to have lunch now. ____ , I want to look inside this clothes shop first.  a) Therefore  b) However  c) Instead → **b** (L50)
3. I’ll be in the office tomorrow. I hope ____ there too.  a) you will be  b) being you  c) you to be → **a** (L51)
4. Dinesh never says hello to us. I ____ him very impolite.  a) see  b) make  c) find → **c** (L53)
5. If you keep ____ trying, you will succeed.  a) up  b) on  c) by → **b** (L54)
6. Not everyone can work ____ a teacher. Teaching teenagers is hard!  a) like  b) how  c) as → **c** (L56)
7. One ____ I’ll speak English perfectly. I just need to study and practise.  a) year  b) day  c) time → **b** (L59)
8. How can you not notice anything different ____ my hair? It’s a completely different colour!  a) for  b) about  c) from → **b** (L60)
9. ____ she knows it’s a really dangerous job, Catherine enjoys working as a detective.  a) Although  b) However  c) So → **a** (L61)
10. When David walked into the living room, I ____ him a drink immediately.  a) offered  b) was offering  c) have offered → **a** (L63)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. Are you sure you want to get marry to George? You only met him four months ago. → **Are you sure you want to get married to George? You only met him four months ago.** (L53)
2. I hope I haven’t losed my phone. I had it in my trouser pocket this morning but now I can’t find it. → **I hope I haven’t lost my phone. I had it in my trouser pocket this morning but now I can’t find it.** (L56)
3. I’ve never swam in that river because there are lots of fish in it, and I don’t want them to touch me. → **I’ve never swum in that river because there are lots of fish in it, and I don’t want them to touch me.** (L58)
4. If you’re not feeling well, just get your bed. I’ll bring you some cold medicine and a cup of lemon tea. → **If you’re not feeling well, just get into bed. I’ll bring you some cold medicine and a cup of lemon tea.** (L63)
5. Which radio station are you listening at? → **Which radio station are you listening to?** (L45, adaptado)
6. How much physical exercise do you make each week? → **How much physical exercise do you do each week?** (L46, adaptado)
7. George hates speaking before public. → **George hates speaking in public.** (L47, adaptado)
8. If you’re feeling tired, I suggest you drinking some coffee. → **If you’re feeling tired, I suggest you drink some coffee.** (L48, adaptado)
9. Why did you decide buying chocolates for me? It’s not my birthday. → **Why did you decide to buy chocolates for me? It’s not my birthday.** (L49, adaptado)
10. I don’t like monkeys. I’m afraid from them. → **I don’t like monkeys. I’m afraid of them.** (L63, adaptado)
