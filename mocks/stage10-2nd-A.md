## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (30)

1. The girl found three eggs inside a bird's ____ yesterday.  (**nest** / house / building)
2. People vote in order to choose the next ____ for their cities.  (**representative** / season / president)
3. The doctor listened to my heart ____ and said I was healthy  (die / swim / **beat**)
4. When you don't keep your promises, you ____ others.  (sorrow / **disappoint** / like)
5. Criminals should be ____ of their acts.  (**ashamed** / sad / proud)
6. The ____ should be kept outside as it smells really bad.  (plate / perfume / **rubbish**)
7. A good bed ____ is normally very soft.  (cover / **mattress** / carpet)
8. It's important to lower your body temperature when you have a ____.  (**fever** / trip / appointment)
9. Smoking inside buildings is ____ in Brazil.  (allowed / permitted / **forbidden**)
10. If I won the lottery, it would be something ____ my wildest dreams.  (after / post / **beyond**)
11. Most people's favorite ____ is ketchup.  (food / **sauce** / drinks)
12. Some women get ____ when they are asked how old they are.  (worried / **embarrassed** / lazy)
13. You'll have to ____ me to school, because I really don't want to go.  (**drag** / cry / fall)
14. Recycling is good for the ____.  (life / death / **environment**)
15. His arm was badly ____ in the car accident.  (bitter / **crushed** / sick)
16. You should study every day, ____ you might fail the test at the end of the stage.  (**otherwise** / if / whether)
17. Sunlight and heavy washing can make the colour in cloth ____.  (messy / ugly / **fade**)
18. Listen to my ____: Do not smoke! It isn't good for you.  (idea / conversation / **advice**)
19. We turn a water tap o in a ____ direction.  (**clockwise** / left / anticlockwise)
20. I always find my mobile ____ the sofa!  (into / **beneath** / onto)
21. ____ Things aren't as bad as they seem.  (**Cheer up!** / Hesitate! / Joy!)
22. I have a ____ room at home for my guests.  (empty / **spare** / addition)
23. My family is trying to ____ sugar.  (**cut down on** / squeeze / pump)
24. Don't be a ____! Stop believing everything people tell you!  (**fool** / smart / stupid)
25. When I stay at a hotel I usually ____ the facilities.  (associate / **take advantage of** / set)
26. The loud noise in the middle of the night ____ the children.  (**scared** / awake / asleep)
27. If you follow the ____ you'll be able to cook that particular dish.  (lead / smell / **recipe**)
28. When someone is promoted at work they usually receive a rise in ____.  (stu / bribe / **income**)
29. She thinks that eating with one's mouth open is ____.  (graceful / **disgusting** / sacred)
30. Jack decided to ____ from his job in order to travel.  (**resign** / shiver / whisper)
