## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. If I ____ the postcode on a letter it won't arrive.  (**leave out** / look forward to / take control)
2. Humans used bows to ____ in the old days.  (whistle / **hunt** / blow up)
3. I need a pair of ____ to cut this paper.  (greases / **scissors** / stamps)
4. People in this country are ____ coffee drinkers.  (**mostly** / totally / entirely)
5. The teacher wrote some notes on his ____.  (floorboard / noticeboard / **blackboard**)
6. ____ people are never satisfied with what they have.  (**Greedy** / Artificial / Available)
7. My mother only lets me smoke on the ____ of the flat.  (**balcony** / railing / statue)
8. I have ____ neighbours who always ask questions about my life.  (**nosey** / tidy / worthy)
9. The cave was full of ____.  (worms / **bats** / limbs)
10. One of the main ____ in São Paulo is “parque Ibirapuera".  (attractives / **attractions** / attracts)
11. Receiving bad news makes me feel ____.  (wonderful / **miserable** / wicked)
12. It isn't always possible to put ____.  (**theory into practice** / practice into theory / theory into practical)
13. Food is the biggest ____ in my daily life.  (ability / **expense** / competition)
14. Job interviews are ____ situations.  (stress / **stressful** / suitable)
15. ____ children usually lack discipline.  (Spoil / **Spoilt** / Practical)
16. I would need a ____ to fix two pieces of wood together.  (**hammer** / scissors / axe)
17. I always pack my ____ with shorts and T-shirts.  (**suitcase** / basket / trolley)
18. I have little money and can't ____ a car.  (polish / **afford** / amuse)
19. The ____ of the crime was full of policemen.  (evidence / spread / **scene**)
20. I'm going to get a ____ from the bank so I can buy my house.  (**loan** / sake / expense)
