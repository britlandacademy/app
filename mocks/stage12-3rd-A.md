# Simulado A · Stage 12 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

It’s our wedding anniversary tomorrow. I can’t believe we’ve been married for almost ten years already! Check when your passport expires. I think you may need to renew it soon. Regardless of all the objections, the president refused to change his mind. I’m afraid the entire conference was complete chaos. None of the staff appeared to know what they were doing. As a rule of thumb, try to get at least seven hours’ sleep a night. My driving instructor told me I was ready to take my test. Nigel was rather grumpy earlier, but he’s really perked up since Ana walked into the room!

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What do we call the day each year when we celebrate something that happened in the past, like a wedding?  a) an anniversary  b) a birthday  c) a holiday → **a** (L191, adaptado)
2. What happens to a passport when it is no longer valid?  a) it expands  b) it expires  c) it explodes → **b** (L192, adaptado)
3. What do we call a house that shares a wall with another house on each side?  a) a detached house  b) a semi-detached house  c) a terraced house → **c** (L193, adaptado)
4. What do we call the person who teaches you how to drive?  a) a mechanic  b) an instructor  c) a conductor → **b** (L192, adaptado)
5. What do we call a long set of steps between two floors of a house?  a) a ladder of stairs  b) a ride of stairs  c) a flight of stairs → **c** (L205, adaptado)
6. What does a little bird do with its wings when it tries to fly?  a) it flaps them  b) it folds them  c) it freezes them → **a** (L195, adaptado)
7. What do we call the practice performance before the first real one?  a) a dress code  b) a dress rehearsal  c) a dress size → **b** (L204, adaptado)
8. What is a skeleton?  a) the skin of a body  b) the hair of a body  c) the bones of a body → **c** (L201, adaptado)
9. What do people believe about a place that is haunted?  a) that ghosts live there  b) that it is very modern  c) that it is full of flowers → **a** (L200, adaptado)
10. What do we call the feeling of being sorry for something wrong you have done?  a) remorse  b) pride  c) relief → **a** (L205, adaptado)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. I forgot my mum’s birthday yesterday, but I’m planning to ____ up for it by taking her to a top restaurant this Friday. → **make** (L197)
2. Ronnie got away ____ many armed robberies before he was finally arrested for fraud. → **with** (L197)
3. I’m planning to ____ on a course to learn how to paint properly; I’ve always felt I had a hidden talent. → **enrol** (L195)
4. Ollie realized his remarks had been out of ____ , and he apologized the next day. → **order** (L196)
5. I’ve lived in London for over ten years now. I ____ on it as my second home. → **look** (L198)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. In ____ of fire, staff members should leave the building by the nearest exit.  a) event  b) occasion  c) case → **c** (L192)
2. Our flight’s leaving from ____ 26, but we’ve got lots of time to get there.  a) Boarding  b) Gate  c) Departure → **b** (L193)
3. Why did the soldiers set fire ____ the train station?  a) to  b) at  c) in → **a** (L199)
4. There were countless times I felt like giving up, but I didn’t. I ____ at it, year after year, until I finally fulfilled my dream.  a) kept  b) drove  c) set → **a** (L201)
5. ____ one, the children went up on stage to receive their prizes.  a) On  b) To  c) One by → **c** (L201)
6. Take the weekend to think things ____ and let me know your decision on Monday.  a) by  b) over  c) up → **b** (L202)
7. I can’t believe you dozed ____ during the speeches at your own wedding reception!  a) out  b) away  c) off → **c** (L202)
8. I was sure my application would be ____ down, but I’ve just been invited for a second interview.  a) marked  b) sent  c) turned → **c** (L203)
9. I know Harry’s plan to open his own restaurant will ____ off; he always succeeds in the end.  a) come  b) go  c) bring → **a** (L204)
10. The holiday was good overall, but the hotel we stayed in failed to ____ up to my expectations.  a) stand  b) live  c) face → **b** (L206)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. I went to the cinema yesterday but, even though, I’m more than happy to go again this evening with you. → **I went to the cinema yesterday but, even so, I’m more than happy to go again this evening with you.** (L194)
2. Could you possibly stop over at the supermarket on your way home and get a few things? → **Could you possibly stop off at the supermarket on your way home and get a few things?** (L197, adaptado)
3. I wouldn’t feel Ken’s remarks about your dress sense too seriously if I were you; I’m sure he didn’t mean it. → **I wouldn’t take Ken’s remarks about your dress sense too seriously if I were you; I’m sure he didn’t mean it.** (L197, adaptado)
4. We’d better use this milk today; it’s going to start turning off pretty soon. → **We’d better use this milk today; it’s going to start going off pretty soon.** (L198, adaptado)
5. Your mother thinks your behaviour was out of order, and I incline to agree. → **Your mother thinks your behaviour was out of order, and I am inclined to agree.** (L199, adaptado)
6. We are highly trained, but our aim is always to manage a situation without needing to resort with physical combat. → **We are highly trained, but our aim is always to manage a situation without needing to resort to physical combat.** (L201, adaptado)
7. Harry is so vague-minded; he never remembers anything I ask him to do! → **Harry is so absent-minded; he never remembers anything I ask him to do!** (L202, adaptado)
8. Michael’s very good with colours. He should test his hand at painting. → **Michael’s very good with colours. He should try his hand at painting.** (L203, adaptado)
9. If you make the right decisions now, you stand making a significant amount of money. → **If you make the right decisions now, you stand to make a significant amount of money.** (L206, adaptado)
10. Nobody played badly in the match but, on the other hand, nobody stood above for special praise either. → **Nobody played badly in the match but, on the other hand, nobody stood out for special praise either.** (L206, adaptado)
