## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (30)

1. Children like to play with their ____ on the wall.  (shades / **shadows** / rays)
2. I´ll go to work ____ after the lesson is over.  (direct / directed / **directly**)
3. Maracanã is a ____ sports stadium.  (**huge** / hedge / hunt)
4. It isn´t true that people always feel ____ when they don´t have company.  (alone / **lonely** / single)
5. My mother would never ____ of my marriage with a criminal!  (approval / **approve** / disapprove)
6. A ____ is a track used for races.  (track / railway track / **running track**)
7. A ____ is normally something that is delivered to someone.  (package / **parcel** / packet)
8. When I'm ____ I prefer to be on a place with proper facilities.  (camp / campsite / **camping**)
9. We can find a ____ on a horse.  (sad / sadly / **saddle**)
10. My team usually gets ____ at football.  (beat / bitten / **beaten**)
11. I think you´re easily ____.  (disappoint / **disappointed** / disappointing)
12. I don´t know the name of the ____ of my town.  (represent / representing / **representative**)
13. I give my child a fixed monthly ____.  (allow / allowed / **allowance**)
14. ____ is a virtue.  (**Forgiveness** / Forgotten / Forgive)
15. Solving mathematical problems is a long ____ for me.  (processing / procession / **process**)
16. To read to oneself is the contrary of to read ____.  (loud / allow / **aloud**)
17. I´d prefer the mountains as a ____ for a picnic.  (pot / **spot** / spit)
18. By a ____ person we mean someone who has poor manners.  (course / curse / **coarse**)
19. ____ food smells horrible!  (**Rotten** / Rot / Hot)
20. Ketchup is my favourite ____.  (source / **sauce** / sour)
21. Jack decided to ____ from his job in order to travel.  (**resign** / shiver / whisper)
22. If you break your ____, you won't be able to walk anymore.  (**spine** / hand / heart)
23. My brothers and I look ____.  (sample / aloud / **alike**)
24. ____ parents make sure their children behave well and do not break rules.  (Graceful / **Strict** / Awful)
25. You'll have to ____ me to school, because I really don't want to go.  (**drag** / cry / fall)
26. Usually a person's shyness ____ as they become more mature.  (**diminishes** / twists / shivers)
27. Kari ____ her mother when she is in trouble or need help badly.  (approves / reports / **turns to**)
28. Listen to my ____: Do not smoke! It isn't good for you.  (idea / conversation / **advice**)
29. His dog receives a ____ each time it behaves in the correct way.  (stroke / **reward** / plaster)
30. Don't be a ____! Stop believing everything people tell you!  (**fool** / smart / stupid)
