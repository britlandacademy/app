# Simulado A · Stage 9 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

A hall is a large room. People usually say “p” instead of “pence”. The three forms of “forbid” are “forbid, forbade, forbidden”. The word “wonder” means to ask yourself questions. The word “slight” means small, and usually unimportant. A heap is an untidy pile. The three forms of “dig” are “dig, dug, dug”. A three-piece suit consists of trousers, a jacket and a waistcoat. My great-grandparents are all buried in the same cemetery. The freezing point of water is zero degrees centigrade. A cat’s foot is called a paw. Why don’t we kill two birds with one stone?

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. I reckon it’s ____ likely to snow later today. → **highly** (L139)
2. How on ____ did the Egyptians build those pyramids, Dad? → **earth** (L139)
3. Desmond stepped onto the bathroom ____ and smiled; he had lost a whole kilogram since joining the gym. → **scales** (L142)
4. I reckon we might as ____ walk home. There’s no bus for at least half an hour. → **well** (L145)
5. I can’t believe we’re flying home tomorrow. If ____ we had a couple of days more to enjoy the beach! → **only** (L148)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. I didn’t want to come to Italy but, now ____ I’m here, I love it.  a) that  b) then  c) than → **a** (L139)
2. I want to make a good impression ____ your parents when I meet them.  a) for  b) with  c) on → **c** (L140)
3. In addition ____ the local currency, this hotel will accept the euro and dollar for payments.  a) to  b) with  c) for → **a** (L141)
4. Don’t ____ attention to anything George says. He doesn’t know what he’s talking about.  a) make  b) lend  c) pay → **c** (L142)
5. After ____ an explanation of what had happened to the police officer, we were allowed to go home.  a) telling  b) making  c) giving → **c** (L143)
6. Getting a good degree would enable ____ your career with more freedom.  a) you to plan  b) you planning  c) that you plan → **a** (L145)
7. Jarmila set her 800m world record so long ago that many people now think it may never be ____ .  a) taken  b) broken  c) achieved → **b** (L147)
8. Some people are capable ____ lying without feeling any guilt at all.  a) for  b) at  c) of → **c** (L148)
9. Don’t ____ your temper with Billy. He wasn’t trying to ruin the sofa; it was a science experiment.  a) lose  b) break  c) throw → **a** (L149)
10. I know it’s bad, but I can’t help ____ . Dad’s such an awful cook.  a) to laugh  b) not laugh  c) laughing → **c** (L156)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

I’m really worried that other people may not consider what’s in your best interest. → **You should make this decision alone, as far as I’m concerned.
