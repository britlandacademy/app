# Simulado A · Stage 8 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

A housemate or flatmate is someone who we live with. Science can provide us with artificial arms, legs, teeth and hair. The articles in English are “the”, “a” and “an”. The word “afterwards” expresses the idea of “after that”. The three forms of “bind” are “bind, bound, bound”. The verb “swear” means to say very bad words. By public services, we mean education, healthcare, public transport and rubbish collection. Malaga is a coastal city in southern Spain with some great beaches. Trains normally run on two rails. We’ve got half an hour to wait till the coach comes. Don’t try to deny it, Amy. I saw you! Dad had a tear in his eye when everyone sang “Happy Birthday”.

1. What’s a stream?  a) a big sea  b) a small river  c) a road → **b** (L121)
2. What do we mean by unsettled weather?  a) weather that’s constantly changing  b) weather that is always hot  c) weather with no wind → **a** (L121)
3. What do bees make?  a) milk  b) wine  c) honey → **c** (L124, adaptado)
4. What does a referee blow to stop the game?  a) a whistle  b) a trumpet  c) a flute → **a** (L125, adaptado)
5. What’s the plural of “deer”?  a) deers  b) deer  c) deeres → **b** (L126)
6. What do we call the word “the”?  a) a noun  b) the indefinite article  c) the definite article → **c** (L130)
7. How many limbs do humans have?  a) two  b) four  c) six → **b** (L130)
8. What do we use to split wood?  a) an axe  b) a spoon  c) a pen → **a** (L136)
9. What do we call a tool we use to hit nails into wood?  a) a saw  b) a hammer  c) a brush → **b** (L138, adaptado)
10. What metal is a tin made of?  a) gold  b) steel or aluminium  c) wood → **b** (L138)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. Mary: “I hate my brother!” Father: “Come ____ , Mary. You know that’s not true.”  a) back  b) on  c) down → **b** (L121)
2. Are you feeling ____ to going out this evening? If not, we can stay at home.  a) up  b) like  c) sure → **a** (L124)
3. Write the address carefully and don’t ____ out the postcode.  a) lose  b) let  c) leave → **c** (L124)
4. We ____ what had gone wrong for a very long time.  a) argued  b) discussed  c) quarrelled → **b** (L125)
5. I love my ____ . She’s a wonderful human being.  a) mother-in-law  b) mother-by-law  c) mother-at-law → **a** (L127)
6. ____ regards travel and accommodation, the company will pay for everything.  a) With  b) In  c) As → **c** (L128)
7. Don’t get a taxi to the airport, John. I’m driving there, so just stay where you are and I’ll come and ____ you on the way.  a) lift  b) pick  c) fetch → **b** (L129)
8. Bob Stanton had always wanted to settle in Australia and, in 1948, he ____ realize his ambition. It was a dream come true.  a) could  b) might  c) was able to → **c** (L131)
9. My work as a police officer involves ____ into dangerous situations that people would normally advise you to avoid.  a) me to go  b) going  c) that I go → **b** (L132)
10. I wish I had ____ up boxing years ago. It’s a great way to stay fit.  a) taken  b) put  c) brought → **a** (L136)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. If Yasmin ran this business by own, without her husband, I’ve no doubt it’d become more profitable. → **If Yasmin ran this business by herself, without her husband, I’ve no doubt it’d become more profitable.** (L122)
2. The grass is looking a bit yellow due from the fact that the last rain we had here was over two months ago. → **The grass is looking a bit yellow due to the fact that the last rain we had here was over two months ago.** (L124)
3. We missed the start of the show. The band have already been playing for about twenty minutes when we arrived. → **We missed the start of the show. The band had already been playing for about twenty minutes when we arrived.** (L127)
4. There’s no point for driving to the beach if it’s going to be cold and cloudy. → **There’s no point in driving to the beach if it’s going to be cold and cloudy.** (L121, adaptado)
5. Are you planning to join part in the summer concert this term, Ms Hodgson? → **Are you planning to take part in the summer concert this term, Ms Hodgson?** (L122, adaptado)
6. I didn’t tell Billy away when he dropped the plates; he was only trying to help me with the washing up. → **I didn’t tell Billy off when he dropped the plates; he was only trying to help me with the washing up.** (L126, adaptado)
7. If you hadn’t insisted for leaving early, I’m certain we would have missed that flight! → **If you hadn’t insisted on leaving early, I’m certain we would have missed that flight!** (L126, adaptado)
8. There’s a definite opinion among young people towards finding employment after leaving school instead of going to university. → **There’s a definite trend among young people towards finding employment after leaving school instead of going to university.** (L135, adaptado)
9. Chickens can fly but, so good as I know, not far. → **Chickens can fly but, so far as I know, not far.** (L136, adaptado)
10. Little then little, our new house is feeling more like a family home. → **Little by little, our new house is feeling more like a family home.** (L138, adaptado)
