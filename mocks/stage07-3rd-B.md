# Simulado B · Stage 7 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

The expression “quite a few” means “a fairly large number of”. A snag is a small problem. A seat is anything that we sit on. By a dull person, we mean a boring person. The words “or so” mean “about”. We recycle waste paper, or throw it away. The three forms of “light” are “light, lit, lit”. The three forms of “quit” are “quit, quit, quit”. Remember to vote in the election next week. I’m afraid I can’t talk to you right now. The twins are so similar that I sometimes mistake one for the other. Don’t let me down; I’m depending on your help.

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. I don’t like being dependent ____ you for money; I want to work. → **on** (L103)
2. The standard of living in the eastern region of the country is, on the ____ , higher than elsewhere. → **whole** (L105)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. The starter and main course were tasty. Then, to ____ , Sarah served us her famous banana ice cream!  a) finish  b) last  c) dessert → **a** (L107)
2. Could you possibly lay the ____ for dinner? You know where everything is.  a) dishes  b) table  c) seats → **b** (L107)
3. I’m going to marry Lisa; ____ is, if she says yes!  a) which  b) that  c) who → **a** (L109)
4. A strong coffee each morning is fine, but it is not wise ____ throughout the day as if it were water.  a) by drinking it  b) to drink it  c) that it is drunk → **b** (L112)
5. The team always do physical exercise first ____ in the morning.  a) chance  b) hours  c) thing → **c** (L113)
6. We’d better inform the police about the gun we found in the garden, ____ we?  a) wouldn’t  b) hadn’t  c) didn’t → **b** (L114)
7. The four brothers didn’t get ____ well, and violence was common.  a) about  b) across  c) along → **c** (L116)
8. I commanded the men ____ the building.  a) to enter  b) they should enter  c) if they would enter → **a** (L117)
9. Is it true that eating oranges helps to guard ____ colds?  a) against  b) from  c) out → **a** (L118)
10. Do you think telling jokes is a good way to ____ the ice at dinner parties?  a) warm  b) clear  c) break → **c** (L119)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

1. Nothing I do is a waste my time; I plan carefully and fill every minute of my day. → **Nothing I do is a waste of my time; I plan carefully and fill every minute of my day.** (L111)
2. Before entering the castle, my colleague Sandra and I are going to divide you all among two groups. → **Before entering the castle, my colleague Sandra and I are going to divide you all into two groups.** (L116)
3. I have the taste for simple but healthy food, and I spend very little time cooking in the kitchen. → **I have a taste for simple but healthy food, and I spend very little time cooking in the kitchen.** (L117)
4. When I lived in southern Spain as a child, I was use to hot weather, but I’m not now. → **When I lived in southern Spain as a child, I was used to hot weather, but I’m not now.** (L120)
5. Marisa shouldn’t spoken to her father like that yesterday; it was rude. → **Marisa shouldn’t have spoken to her father like that yesterday; it was rude.** (L102, adaptado)
6. I wanted to get a car but didn’t buy anything at the end. I don’t really need one. → **I wanted to get a car but didn’t buy anything in the end. I don’t really need one.** (L103, adaptado)
7. Bob hates the food here in Spain, and he doesn’t like the weather too. → **Bob hates the food here in Spain, and he doesn’t like the weather either.** (L104, adaptado)
8. I’m afraid I can’t talk to you right moment. Can I call you back in five minutes? → **I’m afraid I can’t talk to you right now. Can I call you back in five minutes?** (L106, adaptado)
9. That was a long run. You can be tired now! → **That was a long run. You must be tired now!** (L109, adaptado)
10. I wish we knew more about this neighbourhood before moving here. → **I wish we had known more about this neighbourhood before moving here.** (L116, adaptado)
