# Simulado B · Stage 9 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

There are ninety degrees in a right angle. Another word for “hardly” is “scarcely”. A dog buries a bone in the ground. European people usually wear black for a funeral. The noun of the verb “admire” is “admiration”. We get water when ice melts. We call a man who works in a mine a miner. We shave with a razor. A voyage is a long journey on water or in space. Almost no crime has been reported in this town in the last year. It’s a complete mystery.

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. Come on, Mum! Dance with the rest of us. You need to ____ yourself go sometimes! → **let** (L141)
2. In my opinion, coffee tastes too ____ without sugar. → **bitter** (L143)
3. I ____ Shaun would stop singing and do something quieter! → **wish** (L144)
4. We should ____ our kitchen knives regularly. They’re more dangerous when they’re blunt. → **sharpen** (L150)
5. Following Roddy’s operation, it was a huge ____ when the doctor told us he was completely fine and recovering well. → **relief** (L156)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. Have you got a rubber I can use to rub ____ that last line?  a) off  b) out  c) away → **a** (L140)

4. Robby grew to love being in the army, ____ all because it was exciting.  a) beyond  b) above  c) over → **b** (L150)
5. You are responsible ____ your own mistakes, Juan, nobody else.  a) of  b) for  c) to → **b** (L151)
6. I just told Tim to go and play in the garden; he was making a real nuisance ____ himself in the kitchen.  a) by  b) with  c) of → **c** (L153)
7. As a secondary school teacher in a tough part of the city, I have to be prepared to ____ my foot down at times.  a) put  b) bring  c) keep → **a** (L153)
8. The letter that’s ____ in the English alphabet is “y”.  a) one before last  b) last but one  c) second of last → **b** (L154)
9. Hugh’s tall, but only in relation ____ the rest of our family; we’re all pretty small.  a) to  b) with  c) of → **a** (L155)
10. If only Jake had ____ more effort at university. I think he regrets wasting his time there now.  a) used  b) made  c) put → **b** (L155)
