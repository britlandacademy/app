# Simulado B · Stage 1 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), Yes/No (15), múltipla escolha (12), correção de erros (8) = 50 pontos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item (“novo” = item novo, no máximo 10%).

## Part 1 · Dictation (15)

The table is large. The box is small. The pen is under the book. The light is on the ceiling. You are sitting on the chair. He is a man. She is a woman. The door is open. The window is closed. There are two pictures on the wall. This pencil is green. That chair is brown. The students are standing behind the table. We are in the room. The wall is low. Anna Brown is short. I can read and write. I can’t speak Chinese. I don’t like television. He speaks French. She goes home after the lesson. Put your hand on your head, please. Close your book. Who is he?

## Part 3 · Multiple choice (12)

1. Mr Brown: “____ is the book?” Mrs Brown: “Under the chair.”  a) Where  b) Who  c) Which → **a**
2. Elli: “Where are Mr Brown and Mrs Brown?” Josef: “____ are standing in front of the house.”  a) They  b) Their  c) Them → **a**
3. David isn’t going to the door; he’s ____ from the door.  a) coming  b) putting  c) touching → **a**
4. Anna Brown isn’t tall; she’s ____ .  a) short  b) high  c) low → **a**
5. Mrs Brown ____ two eyes.  a) have  b) got  c) has → **c**
6. Robert and Alessia ____ speak German.  a) doesn’t  b) don’t  c) aren’t → **b**
7. Walter ____ German with his family.  a) speak  b) speaking  c) speaks → **c**
8. Jack ____ reading a book now.  a) don’t  b) isn’t  c) doesn’t → **b**
9. 1,000 =  a) a hundred  b) a thousand  c) a million → **b**
10. seven, ____ , ____ , ten  a) eight, nine  b) six, nine  c) five, eight → **a**
11. The Brown family are standing in front of a house. It isn’t your house; it’s ____ house.  a) them  b) their  c) they → **b**
12. Jack writes with his left hand, but Anna writes with her ____ hand.  a) right  b) low  c) high → **a**

## Part 4 · Error correction (8)

1. Does he go to home after the lesson? → **Does he go home after the lesson?** (L11)
2. Robert and Alessia doesn’t speak German. → **Robert and Alessia don’t speak German.** (L11)
3. There is three books on that table. → **There are three books on that table.** (L5)
4. Please translate these English words to French. → **Please translate these English words into French.** (L9)
5. The teacher is asking questions and the students are answer them. → **The teacher is asking questions and the students are answering them.** (L10)
6. She dislike television. → **She dislikes television.** (L12)
7. It isn’t your house; it’s they house. → **It isn’t your house; it’s their house.** (L7)
8. Am I touching the wall? Yes, you touching the wall. → **Am I touching the wall? Yes, you’re touching the wall.** (L7)
