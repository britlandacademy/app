# Stage 8 · Mock A · 2nd edition (Exam Book, Test A)

Fonte: Exam Book, Stage 8 Exam - Test A. Parte oral (professor) fora do app. Tempo: 40 min.

## Part 1 · Yes / No quiz (20)

1. Is there anything wrong with this sentence: “He goes always to the cinema on Saturday”?  **Yes**
2. Do employers like punctual employees?  **Yes**
3. Can a small child be trusted with a gun?  **No**
4. Would you mind if you were kicked hard by a horse?  **Yes**
5. Are discussions generally unpleasant experiences?  **No**
6. Do most countries have a big celebration on New Year's Eve?  **Yes**
7. Do athletes sometimes get injured?  **Yes**
8. Do we ring a doorbell after entering someone's house?  **No**
9. Can we split wood with an axe?  **Yes**
10. Were you conscious during the last lesson?  **Yes**
11. Are you pretending to do this exam?  **No**
12. Is a plain uniform composed of many colours?  **No**
13. Can people dress as they like on formal occasions?  **No**
14. Would it be convenient for you to come to school at 4 a.m.?  **No**
15. Do we have to put a stamp on an envelope before posting a letter?  **Yes**
16. Can science provide us with an artificial brain?  **No**
17. Can you afford a Rolls Royce?  **No**
18. Are greedy people ever satisfied with what they have?  **No**
19. Does a surgeon need to have a steady hand?  **Yes**
20. Is a business partner someone you work with?  **Yes**

## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. Children sometimes ____ to be adults.  (**pretend** / chose / argue)
2. Due to the heavy rain I ____ of the car!  (took control / **lost control** / look forward to)
3. The two men had a big ____ and said unpleasant things to each other .  (discussion / argument / **quarrel**)
4. The old house was dirty and full of ____.  (**dust** / powder / kisses)
5. A surgeon must have ____ hands.  (**steady** / artificial / greedy)
6. During autumn the ____ fall from trees.  (**leaves** / shelves / loaves)
7. The police use dogs when they are ____ for a criminal.  (**searching** / supporting / dismissing)
8. The teacher's book ____ from the students book.  (**differs** / insists / equals)
9. People go to ____ in a church.  (**worship** / seize / whistle)
10. We can help ____ accidents on the roads by driving carefully.  (pick up / **prevent** / furnish)
11. The ____ was very sad at her husband's funeral.  (niece / **widow** / landlady)
12. This food smells bad! I think it's ____.  (up to date / **out of date** / fresh)
13. I had to use ____ to kill the rats in my house.  (**poison** / worm / foxes)
14. Everybody looked at the ____ woman as she walked along the street.  (attract / attraction / **attractive**)
15. I get very ____ when I watch a sad movie.  (emotion / **emotional** / funny)
16. I finished first in the running ____.  (compete / celebration / **competition**)
17. That machine is very ____! It uses little energy!  (suitable / **efficient** / bad)
18. Due to bad weather the football game had to be ____.  (harvested / declared / **postponed**)
19. My parents taught me to ____ my food with other people.  (throw / **share** / attend)
20. She hates being ____ during her studies!  (**disturbed** / kissed / declared)
