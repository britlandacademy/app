# Simulado B · Stage 11 (3rd edition)

Mesma estrutura da prova oficial: ditado (15), listening (10), gap (5), múltipla escolha (10), correção de erros (10) = 50 pontos, 45 minutos. Entre parênteses: a lição do livro de onde vem o item. “adaptado” = frase do livro com um erro inserido.

## Part 2 · Listening (10), perguntas lidas em voz alta pelo professor

1. What do clothes do when they get smaller in the wash?  a) they stretch  b) they burst  c) they shrink → **c** (L177, adaptado)
2. What do we call a very quick look at something?  a) a stare  b) a glimpse  c) a wink → **b** (L181, adaptado)
3. What do you wear to stop people from recognizing you?  a) a disguise  b) a uniform  c) a suit → **a** (L188, adaptado)
4. What are the three forms of “wind”?  a) wind, winded, winded  b) wind, wound, wound  c) wind, wind, wound → **b** (L187)
5. What do you do if your foot catches on something and you fall?  a) you float  b) you swim  c) you trip → **c** (L189, adaptado)
6. Where do we go when we leave a boat and arrive on land?  a) ashore  b) overboard  c) underwater → **a** (L190, adaptado)
7. What does a boat do if it has no power and nobody is steering?  a) it races  b) it drifts  c) it explodes → **b** (L190, adaptado)
8. What do we drop from a boat to stop it moving?  a) a wheel  b) an anchor  c) a pole → **b** (L190, adaptado)
9. What do we use sacks for?  a) transporting and storing things like vegetables  b) writing letters  c) cutting wood → **a** (L178)
10. What can we see only when there are gaps in the clouds?  a) the sea  b) the road  c) the stars → **c** (L180, adaptado)

## Part 3 · Gap fill (5)

1. I tried ____ vain to persuade Geoff that Sonia was no good for him. → **in** (L181)
2. The security ____ at the airport meant that our flight got cancelled. I was so annoyed. → **alert** (L182)
3. I called Anne immediately after my accident but, strangely, when I told her what had happened, she just ____ out laughing! → **burst** (L184)
4. Chris: “May I use the torch on your phone for a second, Megan?” Megan: “By all ____ , Chris. Here you are.” → **means** (L186)
5. If you expect me to ____ a blind eye to what you’ve just done, you must be mad! → **turn** (L188)

## Part 4 · Multiple choice (10)

1. Please ensure ____ it to the church on time.  a) to make  b) making  c) you make → **c** (L175)
2. How can they hope to govern effectively if they don’t discuss urgent matters ____ a daily basis?  a) on  b) with  c) to → **a** (L175)
3. Jan was sentenced ____ two years in prison for something that isn’t even a crime in our country!  a) with  b) to  c) for → **b** (L176)
4. Justin realized that, by ____ out the order his commanding officer had just given him, he’d be committing murder.  a) carrying  b) bearing  c) standing → **a** (L178)
5. How can you be surprised? Of course you ____ the sack! You poured coffee over your boss!  a) had  b) caught  c) got → **c** (L178)
6. They weren’t offering him much money, so he decided it wasn’t really worth ____ taking on the work.  a) the while  b) while  c) his while → **c** (L179)
7. When Luca saw the police come in, he made straight ____ the exit, but they managed to catch him.  a) for  b) along  c) on → **a** (L182)
8. Coming back home from the party on your own is completely out of the ____ , Tom. I’ll come to fetch you.  a) matter  b) discussion  c) question → **c** (L184)
9. Kim is a very sensible person except when it ____ to relationships!  a) goes  b) turns  c) comes → **c** (L185)
10. I can’t believe you trusted Charles when he joined the company. I saw ____ him immediately.  a) through  b) by  c) over → **a** (L186)

## Part 5 · Error correction (10)

2. Harry was making no friends at college, but it never occurred in him that it might be his fault. → **Harry was making no friends at college, but it never occurred to him that it might be his fault.** (L177, adaptado)
3. All staff members are entitled for a 25% discount on company products. → **All staff members are entitled to a 25% discount on company products.** (L178, adaptado)
4. Put these biscuits on the table now, and I’ll keep the rest as reserve for when Dad gets home later. → **Put these biscuits on the table now, and I’ll keep the rest in reserve for when Dad gets home later.** (L180, adaptado)
5. Please dispose away all your rubbish carefully, using the bins provided. → **Please dispose of all your rubbish carefully, using the bins provided.** (L181, adaptado)
6. I’d have been late if you hadn’t taken me a lift this morning. → **I’d have been late if you hadn’t given me a lift this morning.** (L183, adaptado)
7. Nothing on this lunch menu really appeals for me. Could we try somewhere else? → **Nothing on this lunch menu really appeals to me. Could we try somewhere else?** (L185, adaptado)
