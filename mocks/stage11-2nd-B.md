## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (30)

1. Denim is a kind of ____ for making jeans.  (**fabric** / clothes / silk)
2. There is a large ____ of bread in a supermarket.  (**variety** / catalogue / shelf)
3. Sometimes my mood ____ during the day.  (falls / **varies** / increases)
4. Some people ____ at funerals.  (laugh / **weep** / smile)
5. A remote control has no use without ____.  (**batteries** / bullets / coins)
6. That teacher had a ____ accent.  (**unique** / narrow / amazing)
7. The girl was ____ with a knife and died.  (threatened / killed / **stabbed**)
8. The runner ____ for air after having finished the race.  (cried / shouted / **gasped**)
9. Sometimes banhado is full of ____ in the morning.  (**fog** / fire / grass)
10. I ran out of clean socks and had to do the ____.  (dishes / **laundry** / meal)
11. Tom was very ____ to be woken up early.  (happy / relaxed / **grumpy**)
12. As soon as I got my first job I started ____ money.  (**investing** / burning / giving away)
13. He was a great person and everyone ____ him.  (loathed / **admired** / kicked)
14. There is fresh orange juice in the ____ on top of the fridge.  (**jug** / vase / jar)
15. Hitler had a very popular ____.  (**moustache** / car / wife)
16. The chef was a complete ____ and didn't cook the food very well.  (**amateur** / professional / authority)
17. Before driving John's car I had to ____ the seat.  (ensure / **adjust** / bump)
18. The old house was ____ and was difficult to clean.  (**filthy** / aware / magnificent)

20. I am almost finished with my book; I'm on the last ____.  (**chapter** / knuckle / fringe)
21. The phrasal verb look out means to ____.  (extinguish something / fade away / **be careful**)
22. The phrasal verb get away means to ____.  (meet for social reasons / spend time relaxing / **go on vacation**)
23. The phrasal verb feel like means to ____.  (reach something / **be in the mood to do something** / visit briefly)
24. The phrasal verb get back means to ____.  (**return from somewhere** / investigate something / be willing to do something)
25. The phrasal verb sell out means to ____.  (an extra special for people watching / **run out of stock** / talk about a subject)
26. The phrasal verb get across means to ____.  (have none left / **communicate an idea** / put on a sample clothing)
27. The phrasal verb set o means of ____.  (**start a journey** / submit a homework or paper / clean the inside of something)
28. The phrasal verb get on means to ____.  (find a solution / **board a bus or plane** / volunteer information about something)
29. The phrasal verb drop o means to ____.  (go on a date / escape from a place / **leave someone on their destination**)
30. The phrasal verb pull over means to ____.  (be careful / attend a meeting / **drive your vehicle to the side of the road**)
