## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. This blouse costs $20 and these shoes cost $50. I can say that the blouse is ____ than the shoes.  (**cheaper** / more expensive)
2. A Ferrari is ____ than a Fusca.  (expensiver / **more expensive**)
3. My mouth is ____ my chin.  (**above** / below)
4. The English drink a ____ tea.  (lot / **lot of**)
5. I go to the cinema a ____.  (**lot** / lot of)
6. We say good ____ to people between about 12p.m. and 5p.m.  (**afternoon** / evening)
7. ____ country is a nation.  (The / **A**)
8. The date today is the ____ of November 2016.  (**2nd** / 2)
9. ____ chair has four legs.  (**Every** / All)
10. I don't ____ my books in the classroom after the lesson.  (live / **leave**)
11. I ____ in São Paulo.  (**live** / leave)
12. She only has breakfast and lunch. She eats ____ a day.  (**twice** / once)
13. Tuesday ____ Wednesday.  (**precedes** / follows)
14. I'm 24 and my brother is 30. I can say that my brother is ____ than me.  (younger / **older**)
15. I'm 24, my brother is 30, and my sister is 32. I'm the ____ in my family.  (oldest / **youngest**)
16. We ____ our coats when we go out in winter.  (**put on** / take o)
17. ____ is either in March or April.  (**Easter** / Christmas)
18. I sit ____ John during the lesson.  (besides / **beside**)
19. Finland is one of the ____ countries in the world.  (hottest / **coldest**)
20. I don't eat ____ food for dinner as I do for lunch. .  (**as much** / as many)
