## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. The ____ of beautiful is “the most beautiful”.  (**superlative** / comparative)
2. This month is May. Next month will be ____.  (April / **June**)
3. We say: it's ____.  (twenty to four a.m. / **three forty a.m.**)
4. We say ____ to people between about 5p.m. and 10p.m.  (good afternoon / **good evening**)
5. ____ country is the opposite of the town.  (A / **The**)
6. She ____ with her parents.  (**lives** / leaves)
7. This piece of paper is ____ than the table.  (light / **lighter**)
8. ____ each student got two eyes?  (Have / **Has**)
9. An onion is a ____.  (fruit / **vegetable**)
10. I sometimes ____ food on my plate after a meal.  (live / **leave**)
11. Ubatuba is ____.  (inland / **by the sea**)
12. You can't lift the table with your ____ finger.  (**little** / small)
13. The four ____ of the year are spring, summer, autumn and winter.  (stations / **seasons**)
14. Fourteen ____ the number thirteen.  (precedes / **follows**)
15. ____ does the lesson begin? The lesson begins at 7 a.m.  (**When** / Where)
16. We use ____ for things.  (**new** / young)
17. We ____ our coats when we go out in winter.  (**put on** / take o)
18. ____ is on the 25th of December.  (Easter / **Christmas**)
19. We can see the ____ in the sky during the night.  (sun / **moon**)
20. We taste with our ____.  (wrists / **tongues**)
