## Part 2 · Circle the correct answer (20)

1. People could die of ____.  (thirsty / **thirst** / water)
2. It ____ me about 30 minutes to get ready in the morning.  (take / **takes** / taken)
3. She ____ her clothes herself.  (choose / chosen / **chooses**)
4. He has never ____ anyone killed in real life.  (see / saw / **seen**)
5. They speak English ____ well now.  (**fairly** / fair / fairer)
6. I'll be back ____ an hour.  (**within** / with / will)
7. I'm in the habit of ____ my mind a lot.  (change / changes / **changing**)
8. The ____ shopping street in SP is “25 de Março St.”.  (**main** / mainly / maintain)
9. Don´t judge others by their ____.  (appear / **appearance** / disappear)
10. You must look both ways before you ____ the road.  (across / **cross** / come across)
11. A soldier's uniform is ____ for him by the government.  (provide / providing / **provided**)
12. The sun ____ late yesterday morning.  (rise / risen / **rose**)
13. The baby had never ____ out of bed.  (fall / **fallen** / fell)
14. Sailing boats move faster in strong ____.  (**winds** / windy / water)
15. Mike is the ____ in his family.  (origin / older / **eldest**)
16. Policemen don't wear their uniforms when they're ____.  (duty / on duty / **o duty**)
17. Suzan finds it interesting to ____ a factory machine.  (operates / **operate** / operation)
18. ____ is at about 06h30am in SP.  (Sun / **Sunrise** / Sunset)
19. A queen is a person who ____ a country.  (**rules** / rulers / ruled)
20. In my opinion the ____ of a good life is to be happy.  (**basis** / base / basic)
